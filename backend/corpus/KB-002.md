## Overview

Sauvage Eau de Parfum is the 2018 version of Dior's best-selling men's line, composed by François
Demachy. It keeps the fresh, peppery idea of the original Sauvage but adds vanilla and star anise, so
it feels smoother, rounder and a little warmer. It's one of three Sauvage versions we carry, next to
the Eau de Toilette (KB-001) and the Elixir (KB-003). Our team recommends it most often to people who
want "the Sauvage smell, but less sharp".

## Notes

- **Top:** Calabrian bergamot
- **Heart:** Sichuan pepper, lavender, star anise
- **Base:** vanilla from Papua New Guinea, ambroxan

## How it wears

The first minutes are bright bergamot with a dry pepper tingle. Within half an hour the anise and
lavender come forward, and the dry-down is soft vanilla over woody, slightly salty ambroxan. On most
of our team it lasted 8–10 hours, with a clear trail for the first three or four. It sits a little
closer to the skin than the Eau de Toilette.

## Who it's for

- Someone who likes the classic Sauvage freshness but finds the Eau de Toilette too loud or metallic.
- A first "grown-up" fragrance that's very hard to get wrong: office, dates, everyday wear.
- Cooler months and evenings, though it still works on a mild summer day.

Who it's not for: anyone tired of smelling Sauvage on every second person, or someone hoping for
something sweet. For a sweeter men's scent, look at Le Male Elixir (KB-008).

## Zoomer's take

The calmer, better-dressed sibling of Sauvage EDT. Same vibe, fewer sharp edges, and the vanilla in
the base is lowkey the best part. Torn between the EDT and the EDP? Get a sample of each and wear
them on different days. Most people end up picking this one.
