## Short answer

Check the parcel at the branch before you leave with it. If the box is damaged, open it there with
the staff. If a bottle is broken, refuse the parcel and ask the carrier for a damage report, and
we'll send a replacement for free. If you only notice a problem at home, write to us within 3 days
with photos or a video.

## At the branch

1. Look at the box when it's handed to you. Dents, tears, wet patches or a strong perfume smell are
   all reasons to check.
2. If the box is damaged, ask the branch staff to open it with you.
3. If the bottle is broken or leaking, refuse the parcel and ask the staff for a damage report.
4. Write to support@zoomerfume.example with your order number and a photo of the report. We send a
   replacement free of charge.

If the bottle inside is fine, take the parcel as usual. And if you chose cash on delivery, refusing a
broken parcel is a refusal with a reason, so it doesn't count against you.

## Found a problem at home

Sometimes the box looks fine and the problem shows up later: the sprayer doesn't work, the bottle
leaks, or it's not the perfume you ordered. Write to support within 3 days of receiving the parcel.
Include your order number and photos or a short video that shows the problem. We pay shipping both
ways and either replace the item or refund it.

## An example

You pick up your parcel and one corner of the box is wet. You open it with the staff, and the bottle
inside is cracked. You refuse the parcel, get the damage report and email a photo of it to us with
your order number. We send you a new bottle at no cost.

## Not the same as a regular return

A damaged, defective or wrong item isn't a change of mind, so the usual return rules don't apply:
we cover shipping both ways, and a defect counts even though you've opened the bottle to find it.
For a sealed bottle you simply don't want anymore, see KB-048.

Our support team works every day from 9:00 to 21:00 Kyiv time and usually replies within 2 hours.
