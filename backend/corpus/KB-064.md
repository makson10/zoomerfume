## Who Zoomer is

Zoomer is the AI helper in the chat on zoomerfume.example. It has been answering customers since June
2025, and it's there 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays.

Zoomer takes care of the everyday questions a shop manager would normally answer: which perfume to
pick, how delivery works, what the return rules are. That leaves our support team free to focus on
the cases that need a person: problems with specific orders.

## What Zoomer can help with

- **Choosing a perfume.** Describe what you like ("warm and sweet for winter", "fresh for the
  office"), name a perfume you already love, or give a budget. Zoomer suggests perfumes from our
  catalog and explains how they differ.
- **The catalog.** Notes, sizes, prices, availability, samples and decants for every perfume we sell.
  If we don't sell a perfume, Zoomer will say so.
- **Shop rules.** Delivery, payment, returns, gift cards, Zoomer Points and promo codes.
- **Notes for later.** Ask Zoomer to remember a perfume you liked, and it saves a note for you, like a
  wishlist.

Zoomer answers from our own pages: product cards, shop rules, the FAQ and our news. When it gives you
a rule or a price, it shows which page the answer comes from, so you can open it and check.

## What Zoomer can't do

Zoomer can't see your orders, payments or account. That means it can't:

- check where your order is or when it will arrive
- place, change or cancel an order
- handle a return or a refund
- check your Zoomer Points balance
- give you a personal discount

For all of these, Zoomer points you to our support team. They're available every day from 9:00 to
21:00 Kyiv time at support@zoomerfume.example (KB-063).

## A friendly reminder

Zoomer is an AI helper, and like any AI, it can make mistakes. For anything important about an order,
the support team's word is the one that counts. If something Zoomer says doesn't match what you see on
a product card or a rules page, trust the page and let us know.

## Your chats

We store Zoomer chats so it can answer your follow-up questions and so we can keep improving the
service. We never sell your data. More about this in KB-058.

## Tips for a good answer

- Be specific. "A vanilla perfume for evenings" works better than "something nice".
- Mention perfumes you liked or disliked before. It's the quickest way for Zoomer to get your taste.
- Ask for a sample suggestion if you can't decide between two scents (KB-052).
