## Overview

Lancôme launched La Vie Est Belle in 2012. It was created by Olivier Polge, Dominique Ropion and Anne
Flipo. It's a sweet gourmand: juicy pear and blackcurrant on top, praline and vanilla underneath, with
iris and patchouli giving the sugar some depth. The "smile" shape of the bottle is a modern take on a
1949 Lancôme design. The line has many flankers, such as L'Eau de Toilette and L'Eau de Parfum
Intense, and people mix them up often. This card is for the classic Eau de Parfum.

## Notes

- **Top:** pear, blackcurrant
- **Heart:** iris, orange blossom, jasmine sambac
- **Base:** patchouli, tonka bean, vanilla, praline

## How it wears

It opens with fruity pear and blackcurrant, then turns sweet quickly. Praline and vanilla are the
stars for most of the day. The iris is in the official heart, but many people barely notice it: it
comes across as a soft, powdery feel rather than a clear note. On our team it lasted 8–10 hours or
more, with a strong trail. It's one of the loudest perfumes in our women's range, so go easy with it
in small rooms. Humid heat makes it wear shorter. On a cold day, though, it can still be there on a
scarf or coat long after you've forgotten you sprayed it.

## Who it's for

- Lovers of sweet, cozy scents who want one that people will notice.
- Autumn and winter, plus cooler spring days.
- Someone looking for a recognizable classic that's easy to like.

Who it's not for: anyone who finds sweet perfumes heavy, or who wants something for summer. For a
sheer, fresh floral, see Marc Jacobs Daisy (KB-020).

## Zoomer's take

La Vie Est Belle is a hug in praline form. It's sweet, but the patchouli keeps it from smelling like a
candy shop. It's so recognizable that strangers will name it before you do. One or two sprays, bestie,
it really carries.
