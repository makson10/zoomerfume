## Summary

You can return a product within 14 days of receiving it if it's unopened and its cellophane is intact.
We refund the product price within 7 working days to the way you paid. Opened perfume can't be
returned unless it's defective. This policy replaced the 2023 version (KB-049) on 15 January 2025.

## What you can return

- Any product with its factory cellophane wrap intact, within 14 days from the day you received the
  parcel.
- Gift-wrapped products. Our gift box may be opened, as long as the perfume's own cellophane is
  untouched.
- Part of an order. Return only the products you don't want; the rest of the order stays as it is.

## What you can't return

- Perfume that has been opened or sprayed, or that has lost its cellophane. We can't resell it for
  hygiene reasons, so we can't take it back. The one exception is a defect (see KB-050).
- 2 ml samples and 10 ml decants.
- Gift cards.

## How to return a product

1. Write to support@zoomerfume.example with your order number and the products you want to return.
   During support hours (9:00–21:00 every day) we usually reply within 2 hours, with a return number
   and our return address.
2. Pack the product in its original box. Any sturdy box works as the outer packaging.
3. Send it to us with Nova Poshta or Ukrposhta. You pay for return shipping, unless we made a mistake
   with your order.
4. When the parcel reaches our warehouse, we check the cellophane and confirm the return by email.

## Refunds

- The product price is refunded within 7 working days after we confirm the return.
- The money goes back the way you paid. For a card payment, that's the same card. For cash on
  delivery, it's the bank card you give us in your return email.
- If you paid in parts, the partner bank cancels the remaining payments and returns what you've
  already paid.
- Delivery costs of the original order aren't refunded, unless the whole order was our mistake.

## Zoomer Points and returns

- Points earned on a returned product are cancelled. Points only arrive 14 days after delivery, so in
  most cases they simply never appear.
- Points you used to pay for a returned product come back to your account.

## Exchanges

We don't do direct exchanges. Return the product you don't want and place a new order for the one you
do. If you're not sure about a scent, try a 2 ml sample before buying a full bottle (see KB-052).

## Examples

- Your bottle arrived on Monday and you changed your mind. You send it back sealed on Friday of the
  same week: you get the full product price back.
- You sprayed a new perfume a few times and don't like it on your skin. It can't be returned. A
  sample next time costs 69 ₴, or 119 ₴ for niche perfumes.
- You opened a sealed bottle and the sprayer doesn't work. That's a defect, not a regular return. See
  KB-050.

## Edge cases

- The 14 days start on the day you picked up the parcel, as shown in the carrier's tracking.
- A return that reaches us with broken cellophane is sent back to you at our cost, and no refund is
  made.
