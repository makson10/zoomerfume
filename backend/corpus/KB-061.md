## Where it started

In 2021, Zoomerfume's entire range fit on Daryna and Oleh's own shelf. The first decants they shared with
friends came from bottles they had bought for themselves. When friends of friends started ordering,
that shelf ran out fast, and the two of them had to decide where the next bottles would come from.
They made one rule that we still follow: we buy only from sources the brands themselves stand behind.

## Who we buy from

Today three buyers look after every bottle we bring in. We work with two kinds of suppliers:

- **Official distributors in Ukraine.** These are the companies a brand has chosen to bring its
  perfume into the country.
- **Brand-authorized wholesalers in the EU.** Some brands reach us through wholesalers in the EU that
  the brand has authorized to sell its perfume.

Before we work with a new supplier, our buyers ask for documents that show the brand has authorized
them. A new brand joins the catalog only once we have a supplier like that. That's also how Lattafa,
Armaf and Afnan joined us in March 2025 (KB-087).

We don't buy from resellers or marketplaces, even when the price looks tempting. We also never buy
testers, bottles without their original box or grey-market goods.

## How we check a delivery

Every delivery from a supplier arrives at our warehouse in Kyiv (KB-062). Before any bottle goes on
the shelf, the warehouse team checks it:

1. We compare the boxes with the supplier's invoice: the right perfume, the right size and the right
   number of bottles.
2. We check that every bottle is in its original box, with the factory cellophane intact.
3. We check the batch code on each box and keep a record of the batch codes in every delivery.
4. Anything that doesn't pass goes back to the supplier. It never reaches a customer.

Our 10 ml decants are made in the same warehouse, from our own full bottles bought through these same
suppliers (see KB-052).

## When a supplier can't deliver

Sometimes a supplier runs out, and we wait for their next delivery instead of buying somewhere else.
That's why some cards say "pre-order": the bottle ships once our supplier's next delivery arrives,
usually in 2–4 weeks (KB-054).

When a supplier stops delivering a perfume to Ukraine, we stop selling it. That happened with Kayali
Vanilla 28 in June 2026 (KB-023 and KB-092). It was a popular perfume, and saying goodbye wasn't easy,
but a bottle we can't trace isn't a bottle we'll sell.
