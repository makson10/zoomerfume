## Summary

Nova Poshta delivers your order to a branch or parcel locker for 79 ₴, or to your door by Nova
Poshta courier for 129 ₴. Either way it usually takes 1–2 days after dispatch. Delivery to a branch
or locker is free from 2,000 ₴, and courier delivery is free from 4,000 ₴. You can pay online or on
delivery, except at parcel lockers. A parcel waits at the branch or locker for 5 days free of charge.

## Options and prices

| Option | Time after dispatch | Price | Free from | Payment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Branch | 1–2 days | 79 ₴ | 2,000 ₴ | online or on delivery |
| Parcel locker | 1–2 days | 79 ₴ | 2,000 ₴ | online only |
| Courier to your door | 1–2 days | 129 ₴ | 4,000 ₴ | online or on delivery |

The delivery time starts when the parcel leaves our Kyiv warehouse. Orders paid by 15:00 on a
weekday leave the same day (KB-041).

## Free delivery

- The free-delivery amount is checked against your products total after discounts, Zoomer Points
  and gift cards. The delivery price itself doesn't count.
- Icon members get free Nova Poshta delivery on every order, whatever the total (KB-056).

## Paying on delivery

At a branch, or to the Nova Poshta courier, you can pay in cash or by card when you collect the
parcel. This works for orders up to 10,000 ₴. Nova Poshta charges its own fee for it, 20 ₴ plus 2%
of the amount, and you pay that fee with the order. Parcel lockers don't support payment on delivery,
so an order to a locker is always paid online. All payment options are in KB-046.

## How it works

1. At checkout, choose Nova Poshta and pick a branch, a parcel locker, or courier delivery to your
   address.
2. Pay online, or choose payment on delivery (branch or courier only).
3. When the parcel leaves the warehouse, you get the tracking number by SMS and email (KB-072).
4. Nova Poshta lets you know when the parcel is ready to collect.
5. Collect it, or meet the courier, and check the parcel before you take it (see below).

## Collecting from a branch or locker

- Your parcel waits 5 days free of charge, at branches and parcel lockers alike.
- After 5 days, Nova Poshta charges a daily storage fee. The fee is set by the carrier, not by us.
- If nobody collects the parcel within 14 days, it comes back to our warehouse (KB-075).
- For a prepaid order that comes back, we refund the order total minus delivery both ways. For cash
  on delivery there's nothing to refund.

## Check the parcel before you take it

Look at the box at the branch or in front of the courier. If it's damaged, open it there with the
staff. If a bottle is broken, refuse the parcel and ask Nova Poshta for a damage report, and we'll
send a replacement for free. The full steps are in KB-050.

## Examples

- Lattafa Yara (1,290 ₴) to a branch: delivery is 79 ₴, because the products total is under
  2,000 ₴.
- Yves Saint Laurent Libre 50 ml (3,690 ₴): delivery to a branch or locker is free. Courier delivery
  to your door is 129 ₴, since that option is free only from 4,000 ₴.
- A Le Labo Santal 33 decant (2,190 ₴), paid partly with 200 points: the products total drops below
  2,000 ₴, so delivery to a branch is 79 ₴.
- Dior Sauvage Eau de Parfum 60 ml (4,590 ₴) with cash on delivery at a branch: delivery is free, and
  you pay Nova Poshta's fee of 20 ₴ plus 2% at collection.

## Edge cases

- Orders over 10,000 ₴ can't be paid on delivery. Pay online instead, for example in 3 interest-free
  monthly payments (KB-047).
- An order placed on a Saturday leaves the warehouse on Monday, so it usually reaches the branch on
  Tuesday or Wednesday.
- If you refuse two cash-on-delivery parcels without a reason, your next order has to be paid in
  advance (KB-041).
- If your nearest Nova Poshta branch is far away, Ukrposhta may be more convenient (KB-044). In Kyiv,
  our own courier can deliver the same day (KB-043).
- This page covers delivery within Ukraine. For other countries, see KB-045.
