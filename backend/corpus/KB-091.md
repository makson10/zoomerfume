## Order by lunchtime, get it the same evening

If you live in Kyiv, your perfume can now arrive on the day you order it. From 7 April 2026, our own
same-day courier delivers orders in Kyiv every evening from Monday to Saturday.

## The details

- **Where:** Kyiv only.
- **Order by:** 13:00, Monday to Saturday.
- **Delivery:** the same day, between 17:00 and 21:00.
- **Price:** 199 ₴.
- **Free:** for orders from 5,000 ₴, counted on the products total after discounts, points and gift
  cards.
- **Payment:** online when you order, or by card to the courier.

Choose "Zoomerfume same-day courier" at checkout. The option only appears for addresses in Kyiv.
Gift wrapping is free with the courier too, so it works well for a last-minute gift (KB-053).

## Why we started it

Many of you order perfume for a date, a party or a trip, and sometimes a couple of days is too long
to wait. Our warehouse is in Kyiv (KB-062), so a same-day courier felt like the natural next step.

## Other options in Kyiv

Nova Poshta still delivers in Kyiv too, to a branch, a parcel locker or by courier to your address
(KB-042). Pick whichever suits your day.

The full rules for our same-day courier are in KB-043.
