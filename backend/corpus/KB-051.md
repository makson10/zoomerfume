## Summary

Every perfume Zoomerfume sells is original. We buy only from official distributors in Ukraine and
from brand-authorized wholesalers in the EU. Every bottle reaches you in the brand's original
packaging with its batch code, and we never sell testers, "unboxed" bottles or grey-market stock. If
you ever doubt a bottle, send us its batch code and we'll send you the distributor's certificate.

## What we guarantee

When you buy a full bottle from us, we promise that:

- it's a genuine product of the brand named on the box;
- it reached us through an official channel: an official distributor in Ukraine or a wholesaler the
  brand has authorized in the EU;
- it's a regular retail bottle in the brand's own box, sealed as the brand ships it;
- the batch code on the packaging is there and readable. We never remove, cover or scratch off
  batch codes.

Our 10 ml decants and 2 ml samples are original perfume too. We fill decants in our Kyiv warehouse
from our own full bottles, the same stock we sell, and our samples come from those bottles as well
(see KB-052).

## Where our perfume comes from

We work with two kinds of suppliers only: official distributors in Ukraine and brand-authorized
wholesalers in the EU. We don't buy from private sellers, marketplaces or other shops, even when a
perfume is hard to get. For more about our buyers and how we choose suppliers, see KB-061.

## Batch codes

A batch code is a short code of letters and numbers that the brand prints or stamps on its packaging.
It links a bottle to the production batch it came from. You'll usually find it on the bottom of the
box, and on the bottle itself, often on its base or label.

Every bottle we ship has one. It's the code to send us if you'd like the certificate for your
perfume.

## What we never sell

- **Testers.** These are bottles brands make for shop counters, often without a cap or a retail
  box. They're not meant for resale, so we don't sell them.
- **"Unboxed" bottles.** Bottles sold without their original box, or with a box that doesn't belong
  to them.
- **Grey-market stock.** Bottles bought outside the brand's official channels, for example from
  resellers abroad. They may be genuine, but nobody can say for sure where they've been or how
  they were stored, so we don't take them.

## How to ask for a certificate

1. Find the batch code on the box or on the bottle.
2. Write to support@zoomerfume.example with your order number and the batch code. A photo of the
   code helps.
3. Our support team replies by email with the distributor's certificate for that perfume.

Support works every day from 9:00 to 21:00 Kyiv time and usually replies within 2 hours. Messages
sent later are answered the next morning (see KB-063).

## Examples

- Your Creed Aventus feels a little stronger than a friend's bottle. Aventus is known for small
  differences between production batches, so this is normal and not a sign of a fake (see KB-028).
  If you'd like proof anyway, send us the batch code and we'll send the certificate.
- You received Baccarat Rouge 540 in a gift parcel and want to be sure it's original. Ask whoever
  ordered it for the order number, then write to us with that number and the batch code.
- The sprayer on your new bottle doesn't work. That's a defect, not a question of authenticity. Write
  to support within 3 days of receiving it, with a photo or a video (see KB-050).

## Edge cases

- When an authorized supplier stops delivering a perfume, we stop selling it rather than look for
  stock somewhere else. That's why Kayali Vanilla 28 left our catalog on 16 June 2026 (see KB-023).
- The batch code on the box and on the bottle usually match. If yours don't, or you can't find a
  code at all, write to support with photos of both and we'll check it for you.
- A gift-wrapped order comes in our grape-and-pink gift box. The brand's original box, with its
  batch code, is inside it (see KB-053).
