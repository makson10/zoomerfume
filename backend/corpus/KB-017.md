## Overview

Valentino launched Born in Roma Donna in 2019 as a flanker of Valentino Donna, and it soon became a
line of its own. It comes in a studded bottle inspired by the brand's Rockstud design. The scent is all
about jasmine in three forms, the flower, jasmine sambac and a jasmine tea accord, resting on creamy
bourbon vanilla and smooth woods. It's sweet and floral, but cleaner and airier than the heavy vanilla
perfumes. There's also a men's Uomo Born in Roma and many limited flankers. This card is for the
original Donna Eau de Parfum.

## Notes

- **Top:** bergamot, blackcurrant
- **Heart:** jasmine sambac, jasmine grandiflorum, jasmine tea accord
- **Base:** bourbon vanilla, cashmeran, guaiac wood

Some lists also add pink pepper to the top.

## How it wears

Tart blackcurrant and bergamot come first, then jasmine everywhere. The tea accord softens it, so it
never gets too heady. The vanilla takes its time and shows up after an hour or two, together with a
smooth, woody warmth. People disagree on how long it lasts. On our team most people could still smell
it at the end of a working day, but for some the trail faded after a couple of hours and it stayed on
as a soft skin scent.

## Who it's for

- Jasmine lovers who want a modern, polished version of it.
- Daytime at work and evenings out, from autumn to spring.
- Someone who likes vanilla but wants a scent that's more floral than dessert.

Who it's not for: someone who wants a strong, room-filling scent. For a jasmine that announces itself,
try Mugler Alien (KB-021).

## Zoomer's take

Born in Roma is that friend who always looks effortlessly put together. Pretty, sweet, never too loud.
The jasmine tea accord is the secret sauce: it makes the flowers feel fresh instead of heavy. Easy to
wear five days a week without anyone getting tired of it, including you.
