**Archived page. This policy is no longer in force.** It applied to orders from 1 March 2023 to
14 January 2025. On 15 January 2025 it was replaced by the current returns policy, KB-048. For any
order from 15 January 2025, the rules in KB-048 apply, not the ones below.

## Summary

You can return a product within 30 days of receiving it. Unopened products can always be returned
within that time. Opened perfume can be returned too, as long as less than 10% of the bottle has
been used. We refund the product price within 7 working days, the same way you paid.

## What you can return

- Any unopened product with its cellophane intact, within 30 days from the day you received the
  parcel.
- Opened perfume, if less than 10% of the bottle has been used, within the same 30 days. A few test
  sprays are fine. A bottle with a clearly lower level is not.
- Gift-wrapped products. Our gift box may be opened.
- Part of an order. Return only the products you don't want; the rest of the order stays as it is.

## What you can't return

- Perfume with 10% or more of the bottle used.
- 2 ml samples and 10 ml decants.

## How to return a product

1. Write to support@zoomerfume.example with your order number and the products you want to return.
   We reply with a return number and our return address.
2. Pack the product in its original box. Any sturdy box works as the outer packaging.
3. Send it to us with Nova Poshta. You pay for return shipping, unless we made a mistake with your
   order.
4. When the parcel reaches our warehouse, we check the product. For an opened bottle, we also check
   how much has been used. Then we confirm the return by email.

## Refunds

- The product price is refunded within 7 working days after we confirm the return.
- The money goes back the way you paid. For a card payment, that's the same card. For cash on
  delivery, it's the bank card you give us in your return email.
- Delivery costs of the original order aren't refunded, unless the whole order was our mistake.

## Exchanges

We don't do direct exchanges. Return the product you don't want and place a new order for the one you
do.

## Examples

- You sprayed a new perfume a few times over two weeks and it's not for you. Most of the bottle is
  still there, so you can return it within 30 days of receipt and get the product price back.
- You've used about a quarter of the bottle. That's more than 10%, so it can't be returned.
- You send back a sealed bottle 25 days after receiving it. That's within 30 days, so you get a full
  refund of the product price.

## Edge cases

- The 30 days start on the day you picked up the parcel, as shown in the carrier's tracking.
- A returned bottle that reaches us with 10% or more used is sent back to you at our cost, and no
  refund is made.
- A defect, such as a sprayer that doesn't work, isn't a regular return. Write to support with
  photos, and we'll replace the product or refund it.
