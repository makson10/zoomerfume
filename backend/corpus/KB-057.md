## Summary

You can use one promo code per order. Promo codes don't work on products that are already on sale,
but they do combine with Zoomer Points and gift cards. Our next sale is Black Friday 2026: 27–30
November 2026, with up to −30% on selected perfumes. Our Valentine's 2026 offer and the 5th birthday
sale with the code ZOOMER5 have both ended.

## Promo code rules

- **One code per order.** A second code can't be added to the same order.
- **Not on sale products.** If your cart has both sale products and full-price products, the code
  discounts only the full-price ones.
- **Works with points and gift cards.** You can use a promo code, Zoomer Points and a gift card in
  the same order (see KB-055 and KB-053).
- **Each code has its own dates.** Once a promotion ends, its code stops working.

### How to use a promo code

1. Add the products to your cart and go to checkout.
2. Enter the code in the promo code field. The discount shows in your order total before you pay.
3. Pay the rest as usual, with points, a gift card or any payment method.

Zoomer can't create promo codes or give personal discounts. It can explain how a promotion works and
which rules apply.

## How sales work

- During a sale, the discounted price is shown right on the product card. You don't need a code.
- You earn Zoomer Points on what you actually pay, after the discount (see KB-055).
- The free-delivery thresholds look at the products total after discounts, points and gift cards
  (see KB-042).
- Icon members get early access to our sales (see KB-056).

## Black Friday 2026

We announced Black Friday 2026 on 10 October 2026 (see KB-096).

- **Dates:** 27–30 November 2026.
- **Discount:** up to −30% on selected perfumes.
- **The list:** the perfumes in the sale go live on our site on 26 November 2026. Until then, the
  list isn't public, and Zoomer can't say which perfumes will be on it.
- **Icon early access:** Icon members can start shopping the sale from 26 November 2026, 18:00.
- **Codes:** Black Friday perfumes are sale products, so promo codes don't apply to them. Points and
  gift cards work as usual.

## Examples

- Your cart has a full-price bottle of Burberry Goddess and a perfume that's on sale. You enter a
  promo code: the discount applies to Burberry Goddess only, and the sale perfume keeps its sale
  price.
- You have two promo codes. Only one can go on this order.
- You enter ZOOMER5 in October 2026. It doesn't work, because the birthday sale ended on 30 September
  2026.
- You're an Icon member. On 26 November 2026 at 18:00 you can buy Black Friday perfumes at their sale
  prices. Other customers can buy them from 27 November.
- You place an order on 28 November 2026 with a Black Friday perfume and pay part of it with Zoomer
  Points. That's fine: points work on sale products, for up to 30% of the products total.

## Edge cases

- **A code doesn't work.** Check that the promotion hasn't ended, that the products in your cart
  aren't on sale, and that there's no other code on the order. If it still doesn't work, write to
  support@zoomerfume.example with the code.
- **Points from Valentine's 2026.** The double points arrived like any other points, 14 days after
  delivery. They arrived before 1 September 2026, so they keep the 24-month term (see KB-055).
