## Who we are

Zoomerfume is a Ukrainian online perfume shop. We sell original perfume from the brands people
actually wear now: Gen-Z favorites, worldwide classics, niche icons and affordable Middle-Eastern
houses. Our motto is "perfume without the snobbery". We describe every scent in plain words, tell
you who it's *not* for, and let you try a sample before you commit to a bottle.

We only sell online at zoomerfume.example. We have no shops, no showroom and no pickup point. Every
order ships from our warehouse in Kyiv (see KB-062).

## How it started

Zoomerfume was founded in March 2021 in Kyiv by two friends, Daryna and Oleh. It started with their
own bottles: friends kept asking to borrow a few sprays, so they began splitting perfume into small
decants for friends, and then for friends of friends. When the waiting list got longer than their
collection, they opened a proper shop.

Decants and samples are still a big part of how we work (see KB-052). Most people don't know what a
perfume really smells like until they've worn it for a day.

## Zoomerfume in numbers

- Founded: March 2021, Kyiv
- Team: 27 people (see KB-065)
- Orders shipped: over 120,000 since 2021
- Catalog: about 40 perfumes, each one chosen and worn by the team before it goes on sale
- Delivery: all over Ukraine with Nova Poshta and Ukrposhta, a same-day courier in Kyiv, and seven
  countries abroad (see KB-045)

## What we sell, and what we don't

We sell perfume only: no skincare and no makeup. Every bottle is original and comes from official
distributors or brand-authorized wholesalers (see KB-061). We don't sell testers or bottles without
their original packaging.
