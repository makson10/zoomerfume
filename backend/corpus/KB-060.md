## Perfume without the snobbery

That's our motto, and it sums up how we want the shop to feel. Choosing a perfume shouldn't feel like
an exam. You don't need to know perfume words or the history of a brand to find something you love,
and nobody here will make you feel silly for asking. Everything we do comes back to four promises.

## 1. Only originals

Every bottle we sell is the real thing. We buy only from official distributors in Ukraine and
brand-authorized wholesalers in the EU. We never sell testers, bottles without their original box or
grey-market stock. How we choose suppliers and check every delivery is described in KB-061, and our
formal guarantee is in KB-051.

This promise sometimes costs us a perfume. In June 2026 our authorized supplier stopped delivering
Kayali Vanilla 28 to Ukraine. We stopped selling it rather than look for bottles somewhere else
(see KB-092).

## 2. Honest descriptions

We write every product card ourselves, in plain words, after the team has worn the perfume. Each card
says who a scent is for, and also who it's *not* for. If a perfume is very sweet, very loud or easy to
overspray, the card says so. We'd rather you skip a bottle than buy one that ends up forgotten on a
shelf.

## 3. Samples before bottles

A perfume can seem perfect for ten minutes and wrong after four hours. That's why samples are at the
heart of the shop, just as they were when Daryna and Oleh started by sharing decants with friends
(KB-059).

- A 2 ml sample of any perfume in stock costs 69 ₴, or 119 ₴ for niche perfumes.
- Niche perfumes also come as 10 ml decants.
- Every order from 1,500 ₴ gets a free sample.
- If you buy a full bottle of the same perfume within 30 days, the sample price comes back to you as
  Zoomer Points.

The details are in KB-052.

## 4. Talk like a friend

No question is too basic, and no taste is "wrong". Our support team (KB-063) and Zoomer, our AI helper
in the site chat (KB-064), talk to you the way a friend who knows perfume would: short, honest and
without fancy words. If we think a perfume isn't right for you, we'll say so, even if it's the most
expensive one you're looking at.

## Clear rules, written down

Friendly doesn't mean vague. All our prices are in hryvnias with taxes included. Our rules for
ordering, returns and Zoomer Points are all published on the site (see KB-041, KB-048 and KB-055).
If something goes wrong with an order, write to support with your order number, and we'll sort it
out.
