## Summary

You order on zoomerfume.example, with or without an account. Every order is confirmed by email and
SMS. Orders paid by 15:00 on a weekday leave our Kyiv warehouse the same day, and the carrier's
tracking number follows by SMS and email. Until the parcel is dispatched, you can change or cancel the
order by writing to support.

## Placing an order

1. Add the perfumes and samples you want to your cart.
2. At checkout, enter your name, phone number and email, then pick a delivery option: Nova Poshta
   (KB-042), the same-day courier in Kyiv (KB-043), Ukrposhta (KB-044) or international shipping
   (KB-045).
3. Add a promo code, Zoomer Points or a gift card if you have them, and choose how to pay (KB-046).
4. Use the order comment for anything extra, such as the free sample you'd like on orders from
   1,500 ₴ (KB-086) or the message for a gift box (KB-053).
5. Confirm the order. The confirmation with your order number arrives by email and SMS. Keep the
   order number: support will ask for it.

## Guest checkout or an account

You don't need an account to order. Guest checkout asks only for your contact and delivery details.

An account, which you sign in to with your phone number, is needed for one thing: Zoomer Points. With
an account you earn points on your orders, can pay with them, and get bonus points in your birthday
month (KB-055). An order placed as a guest earns no points. More in KB-083.

## When your order leaves the warehouse

Every order ships from our warehouse in Kyiv (KB-062). It leaves on these terms:

- Paid by 15:00, Monday to Friday: it leaves the same day.
- Paid after 15:00 on a weekday: it leaves the next working day.
- Placed on Saturday or Sunday: it leaves on Monday.
- Public holidays move dispatch to the next working day.

For cash on delivery, what counts is the time your order is confirmed, since nothing is paid at
checkout. A pay-in-parts order counts as paid as soon as the bank approves it (KB-047).

Two kinds of orders follow their own timing. The same-day courier in Kyiv has an earlier cut-off,
13:00 from Monday to Saturday (KB-043). Pre-order products leave when the stock arrives, usually in
2–4 weeks (KB-054).

After dispatch, Nova Poshta takes 1–2 days, Ukrposhta 3–5 days, and international parcels 5–10 days.

## Tracking

As soon as your parcel leaves the warehouse, the carrier's tracking number comes to you by SMS and
email. Use it with the carrier to follow the parcel (KB-072). If it seems stuck, see KB-074.

## Changing or cancelling an order

You can change or cancel an order until it's dispatched. Write to support@zoomerfume.example with your
order number and what you'd like to change, for example the delivery address, a product or the
delivery option. If you cancel a prepaid order, we refund the full amount to the way you paid.

An order paid by 15:00 on a weekday leaves the same day, so write as early as you can. Support works
every day from 9:00 to 21:00 Kyiv time and usually replies within 2 hours (KB-063).

Once the parcel is on its way, the order can't be changed. You can still return unopened products
within 14 days of receiving them (KB-048).

## Refusing a cash-on-delivery parcel

A parcel with cash on delivery can be refused at the Nova Poshta branch. If you refuse two parcels
without a reason, your next order has to be paid in advance. Refusing a parcel because the box or
bottle is damaged is a refusal with a reason (KB-050).

## How much you can order

There's no limit on the size of an order. Two things have their own caps: samples, up to 5 per order
(KB-052), and cash on delivery, for orders up to 10,000 ₴ (KB-046).

## Zoomer and your order

Zoomer, the AI helper in the site chat, can help you choose a perfume and explain these rules. It
can't see your orders, place an order for you or change one (KB-064). For anything about a specific
order, write to support.

## Examples

- You pay by card at 14:40 on a Tuesday. The order leaves on Tuesday, and with Nova Poshta it usually
  reaches the branch on Wednesday or Thursday.
- You pay at 15:20 on a Thursday. The order leaves on Friday.
- You place an order at 16:00 on a Friday, or at any time on Saturday. It leaves on Monday.
- You paid at 10:00 on a Wednesday and want a bigger bottle instead. Write to support right away. If
  the parcel has already left, the order stays as it is, and you can return the unopened bottle
  after it arrives.

## Edge cases

- A public holiday on a weekday counts like a weekend day: orders placed then leave on the next
  working day.
- Sending a perfume to someone else? Enter their delivery details and pay in advance, so they pay
  nothing on delivery (KB-080).
- A prepaid Nova Poshta parcel that nobody collects comes back to us after 14 days. We refund it
  minus the delivery both ways (KB-075).
