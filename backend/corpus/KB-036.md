## Overview

Gypsy Water is one of Byredo's signature scents. It was created by perfumer Jérôme Epinette and
launched in 2008. It mixes fresh pine and juniper with soft sandalwood, vanilla and a thread of
incense. Byredo calls it genderless and describes its intensity as medium, and that's exactly how it
wears: clean, calm and close to the skin. This card is for the classic Eau de Parfum. Byredo also
makes a stronger Gypsy Water Absolu, which we don't carry. It's one of the niche perfumes we also
sell as a 10 ml decant.

## Notes

- **Top:** bergamot, juniper berries, lemon, pepper
- **Heart:** incense, orris, pine needle
- **Base:** amber, sandalwood, vanilla

## How it wears

It starts bright and zesty: lemon, bergamot and a peppery juniper snap. After about 20 minutes the
pine needles and a light, dry incense come in, and it feels like a sunny clearing in a forest. The
dry-down is creamy sandalwood with a soft vanilla glow, never sugary. It's a quiet scent. On our
team it lasted 4–6 hours. After the first hour it stayed close to the skin, noticeable mostly to
people standing near you.

## Who it's for

- Someone who wants a clean, woody scent that won't bother anyone at the office.
- People who like fresh scents but find most citrus and "blue" perfumes too generic.
- Spring and autumn days, plus cool summer evenings.

Who it's not for: anyone who wants big projection or a scent that lasts from morning to night. For
more presence and longer wear in a similar woody style, try Le Labo Santal 33 (KB-033).

## Zoomer's take

Main character walking through a pine forest: green, a bit smoky, and soft and creamy underneath.
It's subtle, so it's perfect when you don't want your perfume to arrive before you do. It fades
sooner than most niche scents, which makes the 10 ml decant ideal to keep in your bag for a midday
spray.
