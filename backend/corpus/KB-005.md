## Overview

1 Million Parfum came out in 2020. It shares the gold-ingot bottle with the original 1 Million Eau
de Toilette (KB-004), but it's a different scent, not just a stronger version. Instead of cinnamon
and amber, it's salty and sunny: creamy tuberose, sea salt and monoï over soft leather. The brand
calls it a "solar leather" scent. The house has been called Rabanne since 2023 (formerly Paco
Rabanne). The women's scent of the same family is Lady Million (KB-006).

## Notes

- **Top:** grapefruit, monoï, sea salt accord
- **Heart:** salty tuberose, pine, cashmeran, cinnamon
- **Base:** labdanum, leather, woody amber, vanilla, tonka bean

Some other lists leave out the grapefruit, vanilla and tonka bean and add ambergris instead.

## How it wears

It starts with a burst of salt and creamy tuberose, and many people say it reminds them of sun
lotion on warm skin. Some love that, some really don't, which is why we suggest a sample first. Over
the next few hours the leather and woody amber come through, but they stay soft. Rabanne promises
10–12 hours. On our team it easily lasted through a long day, though after the first few hours it
sits closer to the skin than the Eau de Toilette.

## Who it's for

- Summer evenings, holidays and nights out in warm weather.
- Someone who likes the 1 Million bottle but finds the Eau de Toilette too sweet and cinnamon-heavy.
- Fans of creamy, beachy scents with a bit of edge.

Who it's not for: anyone who dislikes tuberose or the sun-lotion effect, or who wants a classic
fresh summer scent. For that, go with Rabanne Invictus Eau de Toilette (KB-007).

## Zoomer's take

The vacation version of 1 Million. Salty skin, sunscreen, a drink at sunset, that whole vibe. It's
very love-it-or-hate-it, so spend 69 ₴ on a sample before you commit. If it clicks, it's a summer
signature nobody else in the room will have.
