## Overview

Eros Eau de Toilette is Versace's bold men's scent, launched in 2012 and created by Aurélien
Guichard. It mixes cold mint and green apple with a thick vanilla and tonka base, so it feels fresh
and sweet at the same time. This is the original Eros. Flankers like Eros Eau de Parfum, Eros Flame
and Eros Energy are different scents that we don't carry, and Eros Pour Femme is a separate women's
line. It's often grouped with Rabanne 1 Million (KB-004) as one of the two classic sweet night-out
scents. It's also one of the easiest ways into our designer men's range: the 30 ml bottle is 1,890
₴.

## Notes

- **Top:** mint leaves, green apple, Italian lemon
- **Heart:** tonka bean, ambroxan, geranium
- **Base:** Madagascar vanilla, Virginia cedar, Atlas cedar, vetiver, oakmoss

## How it wears

The start is cold mint and crunchy green apple, almost fizzy. Within an hour it melts into creamy
vanilla and tonka, with cedar holding everything together. On our team it lasted 7–9 hours. It's
loud: the trail is strong for the first 2–3 hours and still easy to notice after that. On a cold
evening the mint feels refreshing, but in real summer heat the vanilla can turn cloying.

## Who it's for

- Nights out, parties, clubs and dates.
- Cooler weather: autumn and winter, plus spring evenings.
- Someone who wants a scent that's fun, sweet and noticeable without costing a fortune.

Who it's not for: hot summer days, quiet offices, or anyone after something subtle. For a calmer
everyday scent, look at Chanel Bleu de Chanel Eau de Parfum (KB-011).

## Zoomer's take

Minty, sweet, loud, zero chill. Eros is the night-out classic for a reason, and it gets noticed in a
crowded room. Two sprays are enough, unless you want the whole party to know you've arrived. Not
sure yet? The 30 ml is a low-risk way to find out if Eros is your thing.
