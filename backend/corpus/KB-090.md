## Double points, 1–14 February

From 1 to 14 February 2026, every order earns double Zoomer Points. It works for every tier: Fresh,
Hype and Icon members all earn twice their usual rate (KB-056).

## How it works

- You need a Zoomerfume account, signed in with your phone number, to earn points.
- Double points apply to every order placed from 1 to 14 February.
- Points are counted on what you pay for products, after discounts. Delivery and the part paid with
  points don't earn points, and neither does buying a gift card.
- As always, points arrive in your account 14 days after delivery.

The full points rules are in KB-055.

## Gifts for Valentine's

Valentine's Day falls on a Saturday this year. Orders placed at the weekend leave our warehouse on
Monday, so plan your gift a few days ahead (KB-041).

- **Free gift wrapping.** Your gift goes into our grape-and-pink box with a card and your message, and
  we never put a price inside (KB-053).
- **Sending it straight to them?** Choose prepayment, and the recipient pays nothing when they collect
  the parcel (KB-080).
- **Not sure about their taste?** An electronic gift card lets them choose for themselves, and you can
  pick the date it arrives in their inbox (KB-053).
- **Not sure about your own taste?** Order a few 2 ml samples first (KB-052).

Happy Valentine's from all of us at Zoomerfume.
