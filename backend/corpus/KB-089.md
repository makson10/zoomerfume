## A new delivery option

From 1 September 2025, you can choose Ukrposhta at checkout. It's a good fit if there's no Nova
Poshta branch near you, or if you'd simply like to pay less for delivery.

## The details

- **Price:** 49 ₴.
- **Free:** for orders from 2,000 ₴. The threshold uses the products total after discounts, points
  and gift cards.
- **Delivery time:** 3–5 days.
- **Payment:** prepaid only. Cash on delivery isn't available with Ukrposhta.

Dispatch works the same as for every order. Orders paid by 15:00, Monday to Friday, leave our
warehouse the same day, and weekend orders leave on Monday. You get the tracking number by SMS and
email once the parcel has left, so you can follow it all the way.

## Ukrposhta or Nova Poshta?

| | Ukrposhta | Nova Poshta branch or locker |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery time | 3–5 days | 1–2 days |
| Price | 49 ₴ | 79 ₴ |
| Free from | 2,000 ₴ | 2,000 ₴ |
| Cash on delivery | no | yes, at a branch |

Need it quickly, or want to pay when you collect it? Nova Poshta is still the better choice
(KB-042). Not in a hurry? Ukrposhta saves you a little on delivery.

## Returns, too

You can also send a return back to us with Ukrposhta (KB-048).

The full rules for Ukrposhta delivery are in KB-044.
