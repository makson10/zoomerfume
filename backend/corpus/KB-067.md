## Short answer

Yes, every bottle we sell is original. We buy only from official distributors in Ukraine and from
wholesalers in the EU that the brands themselves have authorized. Each bottle reaches you in its
original packaging, with a batch code.

## What we never sell

- Testers (see KB-068).
- "Unboxed" bottles that have lost their original packaging.
- Grey-market stock, meaning bottles that came through unofficial channels.

This is also why a perfume is sometimes on pre-order with us: we wait for the official supply
instead of buying from someone else. And when an authorized supplier stops delivering a perfume to
Ukraine, we stop selling it. That's what happened with Kayali Vanilla 28 (KB-023).

## What about samples and decants?

Our 2 ml samples and 10 ml decants are filled in our Kyiv warehouse from our own full bottles, the
same stock we sell. A sample is the same perfume you'd get in the full bottle, just less of it.

## How to check your bottle

1. Look at the packaging. It's the brand's own box, sealed in its factory cellophane.
2. Find the batch code on the packaging.
3. Still not sure? Write to support@zoomerfume.example with your order number and the batch code.
   We'll send you the distributor's certificate.

Our support team works every day from 9:00 to 21:00 Kyiv time and usually replies within 2 hours.

## An example

A friend says your new bottle looks a little different from the one they bought on holiday, and now
you're worried. Take a photo of the batch code on your box and email it to support with your order
number. We reply with the distributor's certificate, so you can see exactly where your bottle came
from.

If the problem is with the bottle itself, for example the sprayer doesn't work or the bottle leaks,
that's a defect, not an authenticity question. Write to us within 3 days of receiving the parcel
(see KB-050).
