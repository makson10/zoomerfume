## What's changing

From 1 September 2026, Zoomer Points expire 12 months after they arrive in your account. Until now,
points lasted 24 months.

## What about the points I already have?

They keep their old term. Points that arrived before 1 September 2026 still expire 24 months after
they arrived. Only points that arrive on or after 1 September get the new 12-month term. Your account
page shows each batch of points with its own expiry date.

For example, points from an order delivered on 20 September 2026 arrive on 4 October 2026 and expire
on 4 October 2027. Points that arrived on 10 August 2026 keep the old rule and expire on 10 August 2028.

## Why we're doing this

Our tiers already work on a 12-month rhythm: Fresh, Hype and Icon are based on what you spent in the
last 12 months (see KB-056). Giving points the same 12 months keeps the program simple. It also lets us
keep the earn rates and the birthday bonus the same.

## What stays the same

- Earn rates: 5% for Fresh, 7% for Hype, 10% for Icon.
- You can use points from 100 points, for up to 30% of the products total.
- 200 bonus points in your birthday month.
- 1 point = 1 ₴.

The full rules are in KB-055.
