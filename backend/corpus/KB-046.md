## Summary

You can pay online by Visa or Mastercard, Apple Pay or Google Pay. With Nova Poshta, you can also
pay in cash or by card when you collect the parcel, for orders up to 10,000 ₴, plus the carrier's
fee. In Kyiv, you can pay our same-day courier by card. Orders from 3,000 ₴ can be paid in 3
interest-free monthly payments. All prices are in UAH and include taxes.

## Paying online

At checkout you can pay with:

- a Visa or Mastercard card
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- pay in parts, for orders from 3,000 ₴ (see below and KB-047)

Online payment works with every delivery option. Payments are handled by our payment provider, so we
never see your full card number (KB-058).

## Prices and currency

- All prices on zoomerfume.example are in Ukrainian hryvnia (UAH), with taxes included. What you see
  on the product card is what you pay for the product.
- Cards issued outside Ukraine are charged in UAH as well. Your bank converts the amount at its own
  rate.

## Paying on delivery with Nova Poshta

You can choose to pay when the parcel arrives, in cash or by card, if:

- you're using Nova Poshta, at a branch or with the Nova Poshta courier, and
- your order is up to 10,000 ₴.

Nova Poshta charges its own fee for collecting the payment: 20 ₴ plus 2% of the amount. The customer
pays this fee, together with the order, at collection. Parcel lockers don't support payment on
delivery, and neither do Ukrposhta or international shipping.

If you refuse two cash-on-delivery parcels without a reason, your next order has to be paid in
advance (KB-041).

## Paying the courier in Kyiv

With our same-day courier in Kyiv, you can pay online at checkout or by card to the courier when the
parcel arrives. The courier doesn't accept cash (KB-043).

## Pay in parts

Orders from 3,000 ₴ can be split into 3 interest-free monthly payments through our partner banks.
You choose this option at checkout, and the bank approves it instantly. You need a card from one of
the partner banks. Details are in KB-047.

## Gift cards and Zoomer Points

Gift cards and Zoomer Points can pay for part of an order. You pay the rest with any method above.

- Gift cards can be spent over several orders. They combine with points and promo codes (KB-053).
- Zoomer Points need an account. You can use them from 100 points, for up to 30% of the products
  total. 1 point equals 1 ₴ (KB-055).

## Which payment works with which delivery

| Delivery | Online | On delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Nova Poshta branch | yes | cash or card, up to 10,000 ₴ |
| Nova Poshta parcel locker | yes | no |
| Nova Poshta courier | yes | cash or card, up to 10,000 ₴ |
| Same-day courier, Kyiv | yes | card only |
| Ukrposhta | yes | no |
| International (Nova Post) | yes | no |

## Examples

- An order of 3,000 ₴ with cash on delivery at a Nova Poshta branch: delivery is free, and Nova
  Poshta's fee is 20 ₴ + 60 ₴ (2% of 3,000 ₴) = 80 ₴. You pay 3,080 ₴ at collection.
- Creed Aventus 50 ml (12,490 ₴): it's over 10,000 ₴, so payment on delivery isn't available. Pay
  online, or in 3 monthly payments with a partner bank's card.
- Lattafa Khamrah (1,690 ₴) to a parcel locker: pay online at checkout, since lockers don't take
  payment on delivery.
- Byredo Gypsy Water 50 ml (8,490 ₴) with the Kyiv courier: pay online, or by card when the courier
  arrives.

## Edge cases

- The 10,000 ₴ limit for payment on delivery is for the whole order, not for each product.
- An order to someone else? Pay online, so the recipient pays nothing on delivery (KB-080).
- Every refund goes back the way you paid. For cash on delivery, it goes to a bank card you give us
  in your return email (KB-048).
