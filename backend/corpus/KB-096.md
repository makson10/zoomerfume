## The dates

Black Friday at Zoomerfume runs from 27 to 30 November 2026, with up to −30% on selected perfumes.
All times are Kyiv time.

- **26 November:** the list of perfumes in the sale goes live, so you can plan ahead.
- **26 November, 18:00:** early access opens for Icon members.
- **27 to 30 November:** the sale is open to everyone.

## Early access for Icon members

Icon is the top tier of Zoomer Points (KB-056). Icon members can shop the Black Friday prices from
18:00 on 26 November, the evening before everyone else. Tiers are recalculated on the 1st of each
month, so the tier you have from 1 November is the one that counts.

## Sale rules

- Promo codes don't apply to products that are already on sale, so a code won't work on Black Friday
  prices.
- You can pay part of your order with Zoomer Points or a gift card, as usual (KB-055 and KB-053).
- Points are earned on what you pay, after the discount.
- Free delivery thresholds use the products total after discounts.

The full promo rules are in KB-057.

## How to get ready

- **Sample now.** Order 2 ml samples of the perfumes you're curious about, so you know what you want
  when the list goes live (KB-052).
- **Save your favorites.** Ask Zoomer in the site chat to keep a note of the perfumes you like, like a
  wishlist (KB-064).
- **Shopping for gifts?** Gift wrapping is free, and gift cards are a safe choice if you're unsure of
  someone's taste (KB-053).

See you on 26 November.
