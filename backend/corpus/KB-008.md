## Overview

Le Male Elixir is the richest and sweetest version of Jean Paul Gaultier's Le Male. It came out in
2023 in the famous torso bottle, this time in dark blue and black with gold, and the brand labels it
a Parfum. Think of the classic Le Male lavender soaked in honey and vanilla. It's one of the
sweetest men's scents we carry. It's not the same perfume as the original Le Male, Ultra Male or Le
Male Elixir Absolu, which we don't carry. Its sibling in our shop is Le Beau Eau de Toilette
(KB-009), a light, coconut-fresh summer scent from the same brand.

## Notes

- **Top:** lavender, mint
- **Heart:** benzoin
- **Base:** tonka bean

Jean Paul Gaultier names just these four notes. Fuller lists also include vanilla, honey and
tobacco, and all three come through clearly when you wear it.

## How it wears

Fresh mint and lavender lead for a few minutes, then it turns warm and sticky-sweet: honey, vanilla
and soft resin, with a hint of tobacco underneath. It's dense and strong. On our team it lasted
10–12 hours and often longer, with a thick trail for the first several hours. Two sprays are enough
for a whole evening.

## Who it's for

- Someone who loves sweet, warm scents and wants to be noticed.
- Autumn and winter evenings, dates and nights out.
- Fans of the original Le Male who want something deeper and longer-lasting.

Who it's not for: summer days, the office, or anyone who finds honey too sweet. For something
lighter from the same brand, see Le Beau Eau de Toilette (KB-009).

## Zoomer's take

Honey-glazed lavender in a sailor torso. It's sweet, it's cozy, it's a lot, and people love it for
exactly that. This is a date-night bottle, not a gym one. If sweet scents are your thing, it's one
of the strongest performers on our men's list. Save it for cold weather, though: on a hot July
afternoon it would be too much for everyone around you.
