## Summary

A sample is a 2 ml spray vial of any perfume we have in stock. It costs 69 ₴, or 119 ₴ for niche
perfumes, and you can add up to 5 to an order. If you then buy a full bottle of the same perfume
within 30 days, the sample price comes back to you as Zoomer Points. Every order from 1,500 ₴ also
gets one free sample. A decant is a 10 ml travel atomizer, sold for niche perfumes only. Samples and
decants can't be returned.

## Samples

- **Size:** 2 ml in a small spray vial. That's enough to wear a scent for a few days and see how it
  changes on your skin from morning to evening.
- **Price:** 69 ₴ for designer and affordable perfumes. 119 ₴ for niche perfumes: Creed, Parfums de
  Marly, Maison Francis Kurkdjian, Le Labo, Tom Ford, Byredo and Escentric Molecules.
- **Choice:** any perfume that's in stock. The sample price is on every product card.
- **Limit:** up to 5 samples per order. You can order samples on their own or add them to an order
  with full bottles.

### The sample price back as points

Liked the sample? Buy a full bottle of the same perfume within 30 days of your sample order, and we
give the sample price back as Zoomer Points: 69 points for a designer or affordable perfume, or 119
points for a niche one. Both orders need to be placed from your Zoomerfume account, so we can link
them (see KB-055).

This rule covers the 2 ml samples you pay for. It doesn't apply to the free sample or to decants.

## A free sample with orders from 1,500 ₴

Every order of 1,500 ₴ or more comes with one free sample. Our team picks one that goes well with
what you ordered. If you'd rather choose, write the perfume you want in the order comment at
checkout. See also KB-086.

## Decants

- **Size:** 10 ml in a travel atomizer that fits in a bag or a pocket. On most people it lasts about
  a month of daily wear.
- **Niche perfumes only.** For designer and affordable perfumes, we sell 2 ml samples and full
  bottles.
- **Made by us:** we fill every decant in our Kyiv warehouse from our own full bottles, the same
  stock we sell (see KB-051).
- **Points:** a decant earns Zoomer Points like any other product.

A sample is for getting to know a scent. A decant suits you if you already like a niche perfume and
want to wear it for weeks before buying a full bottle, or want a travel size of a scent you love.

## Returns

Samples and decants can't be returned, even unopened (see KB-048). If a decant or a sample arrives
leaking, or its sprayer doesn't work, that's a defect: write to support within 3 days of receiving
it (see KB-050).

## Examples

- You can't decide between Libre and Libre Intense (KB-012, KB-013). You order a sample of each for
  69 ₴ apiece and wear them on different days. Two weeks later you buy Libre Intense 50 ml, and 69
  points come back to your account.
- A Baccarat Rouge 540 sample costs 119 ₴. If you buy the 35 ml bottle within 30 days, you get 119
  points back.
- You love Tobacco Vanille and travel a lot. The 10 ml decant costs 3,490 ₴ and goes in your bag.
- Your order of Sauvage Eau de Parfum 60 ml (4,590 ₴) is over 1,500 ₴, so it comes with a free
  sample. You write "Sauvage Elixir, please" in the order comment, and that's the one we add.

## Edge cases

- A sample bought as a guest can't be linked to a later bottle, so sign in before you order
  samples.
- 5 paid samples is the most one order can have.
- Perfumes on pre-order get samples once their stock arrives.
