## Short answer

Yes. At checkout, enter the recipient's name and delivery details, add our free gift wrapping, and
choose prepayment so they don't pay anything when the parcel arrives. We never put the price inside
a gift parcel.

## Gift wrapping

Gift wrapping is free. We pack the perfume in our grape-and-pink gift box and add a card with your
message. Just write the message at checkout. The perfume keeps its own factory cellophane inside the
box.

## Getting it to them

- **Pay in advance.** With cash on delivery, the recipient would pay at the branch. Prepay by card,
  Apple Pay or Google Pay instead.
- **Tell them a parcel is coming.** A Nova Poshta parcel waits 5 days free of charge at the branch
  or parcel locker. After 14 days it comes back to us (see KB-075).
- **In Kyiv and short on time?** Our same-day courier delivers the same evening, between 17:00 and
  21:00, for orders placed by 13:00, Monday to Saturday (see KB-043).
- **Abroad?** We ship to seven countries (see KB-045). Delivery is prepaid, and customs fees aren't
  part of our price.

## Not sure what they like?

Send an electronic gift card instead. It comes in 500, 1,000, 2,000, 3,000 or 5,000 ₴, and we email
it to you or straight to the recipient on the date you choose. It's valid for 12 months and can be
spent over several orders.

## An example

Your friend's birthday is on Friday. On Tuesday morning you order a bottle, enter her name and her
nearest Nova Poshta branch, add gift wrapping with a short message and pay by card. The parcel
leaves our warehouse that day and usually reaches her branch on Wednesday or Thursday. She opens a
grape-and-pink box with your card inside and no price anywhere.

## If the perfume isn't right

A gift can be returned like any other order: unopened, with the cellophane intact, within 14 days of
receipt. Our gift box may be opened, but the perfume's cellophane may not. To start a return, write
to support@zoomerfume.example with the order number (see KB-048).
