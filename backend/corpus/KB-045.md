## Summary

Outside Ukraine, we ship to seven countries: Poland, the Czech Republic, Germany, Lithuania, Latvia,
Estonia and Moldova. Nova Post delivers the parcel in 5–10 days after dispatch, for a flat 590 ₴.
International delivery is never free, and it's prepaid only. Customs duties and taxes in the
destination country are paid by the customer. We don't ship to any other country.

## Where we ship

International delivery is available to addresses in these countries only:

- Poland
- Czech Republic
- Germany
- Lithuania
- Latvia
- Estonia
- Moldova

Other countries aren't served. If the address is outside Ukraine and outside this list, we can't
deliver there, and we can't make an exception for a single parcel.

## Why only these seven

Perfume contains alcohol, so we send parcels abroad by ground transport only, never by air. These
seven countries are the ones where we can deliver by road reliably. That's also why delivery takes
longer than within Ukraine.

- The price is the same for every destination on the list and for any number of bottles in the
  parcel.
- It's never free, whatever the products total. The free-delivery amounts for Ukraine don't apply
  abroad, and neither does the free Nova Poshta delivery for Icon members (KB-056).
- Dispatch follows the usual schedule: paid by 15:00 on a weekday, the parcel leaves our Kyiv
  warehouse the same day (KB-041). The 5–10 days start from there.

## Paying

International parcels are always paid in advance. You can pay online by card (Visa or Mastercard),
Apple Pay or Google Pay. Gift cards and Zoomer Points can cover part of the total (KB-046). Cash on
delivery isn't available abroad.

All our prices are in UAH. A card issued outside Ukraine is charged in UAH too, and your bank
converts the amount at its own rate.

## Customs

A parcel from Ukraine crosses a customs border on its way to each of these countries.

- Customs duties, import taxes and any customs handling fees aren't included in our prices or in the
  590 ₴ delivery.
- They're paid by the customer, not by Zoomerfume. The amounts are set by the destination country.
- If you're sending a parcel to someone else, let them know they may be asked to pay these fees before
  the parcel is handed over.

## Sending a perfume to someone abroad

You can pay from Ukraine and enter a recipient's address in any of the seven countries. Their name,
address and a phone number the carrier can reach go into the delivery details at checkout. A free
gift box is available, and no price goes inside a gift parcel (KB-053). Customs fees can still apply
on arrival, as described above.

The recipient can also shop on zoomerfume.example themselves and pay with a card from their own
country.

## Tracking

When the parcel leaves the warehouse, the Nova Post tracking number comes by SMS and email. Use it to
follow the parcel all the way to the destination (KB-072).

## Examples

- Yves Saint Laurent Black Opium 50 ml (3,890 ₴) to Lithuania: you pay 3,890 ₴ plus 590 ₴ delivery,
  online. Customs fees, if any, come on top.
- Creed Aventus 100 ml (17,890 ₴) to Germany: delivery is still 590 ₴. The bigger total doesn't make
  it free.
- A parcel to Moldova with payment on delivery: not available. Pay online at checkout instead.
- A parcel to a country that isn't on the list: we can't deliver there.

## Edge cases

- Returns from abroad follow the usual rules: unopened, within 14 days of receipt, and you pay the
  return shipping (KB-048).
- If the parcel arrives damaged, check it at pickup and see KB-050.
- Paying with a card from abroad for an address in Ukraine? Then the usual delivery options and
  prices for Ukraine apply (KB-042, KB-044, KB-043).
