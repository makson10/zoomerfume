## Three new brands, four new perfumes

Three Middle-Eastern houses are joining Zoomerfume: Lattafa, Armaf and Afnan. They make some of the
most talked-about perfumes of the last few years, and they cost a fraction of what a niche bottle
does. Like everything we sell, they come from authorized suppliers (KB-051), and our team wore
them before they went on sale.

## What's new

- **Lattafa Khamrah (KB-037).** A warm, spiced blend of dates, cinnamon and praline over vanilla.
  Unisex, and made for autumn and winter evenings.
- **Lattafa Yara (KB-038).** Soft and creamy: tropical fruit and powdery heliotrope over vanilla musk.
  Easy to wear every day, all year round.
- **Armaf Club de Nuit Intense Man (KB-039).** Lemon and pineapple on top, smoky birch underneath. If
  you know Creed Aventus (KB-028), you've probably heard the two compared.
- **Afnan 9PM (KB-040).** Sweet apple, cinnamon and lavender on a creamy vanilla base. Loud in the
  best way, for cold nights out.

## Why these four

One request kept landing in our inbox: "something that smells expensive, without the price tag".
We sampled a lot of affordable perfumes, and these four were the ones the team agreed on. Each has a
clear character of its own, and they're a great way to explore a style before you spend more on it.
