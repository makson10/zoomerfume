## Overview

Black Opium arrived in 2014 and quickly became one of the best-known Yves Saint Laurent perfumes. It
was composed by Nathalie Lorson, Marie Salamagne, Olivier Cresp and Honorine Blanc. Its signature is
coffee: a dark, slightly bitter coffee accord over vanilla, with white flowers softening the middle.
It shares only its name with the original Opium from 1977, a very different spicy scent that we don't
sell. The many flankers with "Black Opium" in the name are different perfumes too. This card is for
the classic Eau de Parfum.

## Notes

- **Top:** pear, green mandarin
- **Heart:** orange blossom, jasmine sambac, cinnamon
- **Base:** vanilla, black coffee, patchouli, ambrox

This is the brand's list. Other sources put the coffee in the heart and add pink pepper, bitter almond
and licorice.

## How it wears

It opens with sweet pear and a tingle of mandarin, but the coffee shows up fast. The heart is creamy
white flowers with a hint of cinnamon, and the dry-down is vanilla, coffee and a little earthy
patchouli. How long it lasts varies from person to person more than with most perfumes: on our team it
was anything from 5 to 10 hours. It's loud for the first few hours, then it sits closer to the skin.

## Who it's for

- Someone who loves the smell of coffee and vanilla and wants to wear it.
- Nights out, dates and parties in autumn and winter.
- Fans of sweet scents who want a darker, more grown-up kind of sweetness.

Who it's not for: summer heat or a quiet office. For something cleaner to wear to work, try Yves Saint
Laurent Libre Eau de Parfum (KB-012).

## Zoomer's take

Black Opium is basically an espresso martini at 11 pm. Sweet, a bit bitter, made for going out. It's
been a favorite for over a decade for a reason. Two sprays are plenty, the trail does the rest.
