## Overview

Khamrah is one of Lattafa's most popular perfumes, launched in 2022. It's a warm, sweet and spicy
blend of dates, cinnamon and praline over a resinous vanilla base, and many fans say it smells far
more expensive than it is. It's marketed as unisex. Lattafa has since released several Khamrah
flankers, such as Khamrah Qahwa with coffee. This card is for the original, the only one we carry.
Lattafa joined our catalog on 12 March 2025,
together with Armaf and Afnan (see KB-087). Its sibling in our shop is Lattafa Yara (KB-038), which
is much softer and lighter.

## Notes

- **Top:** cinnamon, nutmeg, bergamot
- **Heart:** dates, praline, tuberose, Mahonial
- **Base:** vanilla, tonka bean, amberwood, myrrh, benzoin, Akigalawood

## How it wears

It opens with warm cinnamon and nutmeg, brightened by a little bergamot. Soon the dates and praline
take over, and it smells like a spiced dessert with a splash of cognac. The tuberose is quiet and
mostly adds creaminess. The base is a rich mix of vanilla, tonka and resins, with a slightly smoky
touch of myrrh.

On our team it lasted 8–12 hours. It projects strongly for the first few hours, then settles into a
moderate trail that stays with you all evening. It's easy to overdo, so start with two sprays.

## Who it's for

- Fans of sweet, spicy scents who want a lot of perfume for the money.
- Evenings, dates and the winter holidays, when all that warmth and sweetness feels just right.
- Anyone who wants to try an affordable Middle-Eastern perfume for the first time.

Who it's not for: hot weather, and anyone who finds sweet scents heavy. For something softer and
lighter that you can wear every day, try Lattafa Yara (KB-038).
