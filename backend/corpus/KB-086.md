## Short answer

Yes, if your order is 1,500 ₴ or more. Every order from 1,500 ₴ comes with one free 2 ml sample. Our
team picks it for you, or you can ask for a specific perfume in the order comment.

## Choosing your free sample

Write the perfume's name in the order comment at checkout, for example "free sample: Burberry
Goddess, please". If you leave the comment empty, our team chooses one for you.

It's a good way to try something new at no cost, or to test the next bottle on your list before you
buy it.

## Under 1,500 ₴?

Orders under 1,500 ₴ don't come with a free sample, but you can buy samples:

- 69 ₴ for designer and affordable perfumes
- 119 ₴ for niche perfumes
- up to 5 samples per order

If you then buy a full bottle of the same perfume within 30 days, the price you paid for its sample
comes back to you as Zoomer Points. For example, you buy a 119 ₴ sample of a niche perfume, love it
and order the full bottle two weeks later. The 119 ₴ comes back as 119 points.

## Good to know

- There's one free sample per order, however big the order is.
- Samples are 2 ml spray vials, filled in our Kyiv warehouse from our own full bottles.
- Samples can't be returned, and that includes the free one.
- Want more than 2 ml of a niche perfume? Many people go for a 10 ml decant next. The price is on
  each niche card (see KB-052).

## An example

Your order comes to 1,990 ₴. In the order comment you write "free sample: Burberry Goddess". The
2 ml vial arrives in the parcel next to your bottle. You wear it for a few days, love it, and order
the full bottle the next week.
