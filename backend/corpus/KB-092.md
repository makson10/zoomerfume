## Why it's leaving

From 16 June 2026, Kayali Vanilla 28 is no longer sold at Zoomerfume. Our authorized supplier has
stopped delivering it to Ukraine, and we only sell bottles from authorized sources (KB-051). We could
probably find bottles somewhere else, but then we couldn't promise they're original. So we're saying
goodbye instead.

Its product card stays on the site (KB-023), marked as no longer sold, so you can still read about the
scent.

## If you loved it

Vanilla 28 was a dense, ambery vanilla with a brown-sugar warmth. Nothing in our catalog is a copy of
it, but these three perfumes share a lot of its mood:

- **Tom Ford Tobacco Vanille (KB-035).** Richer and deeper: vanilla with tobacco leaf, cocoa and dried
  fruit. A niche perfume, and the closest match for Vanilla 28's darker side.
- **Lattafa Khamrah (KB-037).** Warm and spiced: dates, cinnamon and praline over vanilla. An
  affordable way to keep that cozy, sweet feeling through autumn and winter.
- **Ariana Grande Cloud (KB-022).** Lighter and fluffier: creamy coconut, praline and soft musk. For
  when you want the sweetness without the weight, all year round.

## Sample first

Each of these is a different take on vanilla, so we suggest ordering 2 ml samples of two or three and
wearing each one for a full day (KB-052). Sizes and prices are on each product card.

Not sure which to start with? Ask Zoomer in the site chat to compare them for you (KB-064).
