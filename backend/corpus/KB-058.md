## Summary

We keep only the data we need to deliver your orders, run your account and answer you in chat: your
phone number, name, email address, order history, delivery addresses and your chats with Zoomer. We never sell your
data. Payments go through our payment provider, so we never see your full card number. To delete your
account and data, write to support, and it's done within 30 days.

## What we store

- **Phone number.** You sign in to your account with it, and we use it for order SMS.
- **Name.** It goes on your orders and parcels.
- **Email address.** We send order confirmations, tracking numbers and gift cards to it.
- **Order history.** What you ordered and when, and how each order was paid and delivered.
- **Delivery addresses.** The addresses, Nova Poshta branches and parcel lockers you've used.
- **Zoomer chats.** Your conversations with Zoomer, our AI helper in the site chat, and any notes
  you ask Zoomer to save for you, such as a wishlist.
- **Birthday,** if you add it to your account to get birthday points (see KB-055).

## Why we store it

- To process, pack and deliver your orders, and to send you confirmations and tracking numbers.
- To run your account and your Zoomer Points.
- To let Zoomer answer follow-up questions. Zoomer chats are stored so it can pick up where you left
  off, and so we can improve the service.
- To help you faster when you write to support about an order.

## What we never do

- **We never sell your data,** to anyone, for any purpose.
- **We never see your full card number.** Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments are handled by our
  payment provider. We only get the result of the payment (see KB-046).

## Who else gets some of your data

- **The carrier you choose.** To deliver a parcel, Nova Poshta or Ukrposhta gets the recipient's
  name, phone number and delivery address. For an order abroad, Nova Post gets the same details. Our
  own same-day courier in Kyiv uses them too (see KB-043).
- **Our payment provider** handles your payment details when you pay online.
- **The partner bank,** if you choose to pay in parts. It handles the application at checkout (see
  KB-047).

## Zoomer and your data

- Zoomer can't see your orders, payments or account. For anything about a specific order, it points
  you to our support team (see KB-064).
- Zoomer never needs your card details, so please don't type them in the chat.
- Notes such as a wishlist are saved only when you ask Zoomer to save them.

## Deleting your account and data

1. Write to support@zoomerfume.example and ask us to delete your account and data.
2. Include the phone number you sign in with, so we can find your account.
3. Your account and data are deleted within 30 days.

Your Zoomer Points are deleted together with the account. Points can't be exchanged for cash or
paid out, so use them before you ask us to delete your account (see KB-055).

## Examples

- Yesterday you asked Zoomer for a warm vanilla scent. Today you come back and ask "is the first one
  you suggested good for summer?". Zoomer can answer because the chat is stored.
- You paid with Apple Pay. The payment went through our payment provider, and we never saw your card
  number.
- You'd like your data gone. You write to support on 3 March, and your account and data are deleted
  by 2 April at the latest.

## Edge cases

- **You ordered as a guest.** There's no account, but we still keep the order details we need to
  deliver it. To have them deleted, write to support with your order number.
- **You sent a gift.** The recipient's name, phone number and address are stored with your order,
  because they're needed for the delivery.
