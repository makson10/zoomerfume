## Overview

Prada launched Paradoxe in 2022. It was created by Nadège Le Garlantezec, Antoine Maisondieu and
Shyamala Maisondieu. It's a bright floral built around neroli and orange blossom, set on a modern,
clean base of amber and white musk, with a touch of pear for sweetness. It feels airy and glowing
rather than heavy. The line has grown since then, with versions like Paradoxe Intense. This card is
for the original Eau de Parfum. It's on pre-order right now (see Sizes and prices).

## Notes

- **Top:** Calabrian bergamot, tangerine, pear
- **Heart:** neroli, orange blossom, jasmine
- **Base:** amber, benzoin, vanilla, white musk

The amber and white musk are modern accords built around newer aroma molecules, which is part of why
the base smells so clean. Some shops shorten the list to just neroli, amber and musk.

## How it wears

It opens sparkling: bergamot, tangerine and a soft, juicy pear. Neroli and orange blossom come in
quickly and stay the main idea, and the dry-down is clean amber with a musky, skin-like finish. Reviews
of its lasting power are mixed. On our team most people got 7–9 hours, but a few only noticed it for
about two. It projects for the first two or three hours, then becomes a soft musky skin scent.

## Who it's for

- Someone who wants an elegant everyday scent for the office and daytime plans.
- Fans of orange blossom who find most versions too heavy or too sweet.
- Spring, summer and autumn.

Who it's not for: anyone who wants a big, sweet scent that fills the room all day. For that, look at
Lancôme La Vie Est Belle (KB-016).
