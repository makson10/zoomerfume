## Summary

From 1 September 2025, you can choose Ukrposhta for delivery anywhere in Ukraine (KB-089). It takes
3–5 days after dispatch and costs 49 ₴, our lowest delivery price. Delivery is free when your
products total is 2,000 ₴ or more. Ukrposhta orders are paid online only: there's no cash on
delivery with this option.

## Price and delivery time

| | Ukrposhta |
|---|---|
| Delivery time | 3–5 days after dispatch |
| Price | 49 ₴ |
| Free from | 2,000 ₴ |
| Payment | online only |
| Where you collect | the Ukrposhta post office you choose at checkout |

The free-delivery amount is checked against your products total after discounts, Zoomer Points and
gift cards. The delivery price itself doesn't count.

## When Ukrposhta is a good choice

- **You want the lowest delivery price.** At 49 ₴, it's cheaper than a Nova Poshta branch (79 ₴).
- **Your nearest Nova Poshta branch is far away.** Ukrposhta has post offices in many small towns and
  villages, so the parcel may end up closer to home.
- **You're not in a hurry.** If you need the parcel sooner, Nova Poshta usually delivers in 1–2 days
  (KB-042).

## How it works

1. At checkout, choose Ukrposhta and pick the post office where you'd like to collect the parcel.
2. Pay online by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Gift cards and Zoomer Points can cover part of the
   order (KB-046).
3. Your order leaves our Kyiv warehouse on the usual schedule. Orders paid by 15:00, Monday to Friday,
   leave the same day. Later orders leave the next working day, and weekend orders leave on Monday
   (KB-041).
4. When the parcel leaves, Ukrposhta's tracking number comes to you by SMS and email (KB-072).
5. Collect the parcel at your post office and check it before you take it.

## Why there's no cash on delivery

Ukrposhta parcels from our shop are always prepaid. If you'd like to pay when you collect the parcel,
choose a Nova Poshta branch or Nova Poshta courier instead. Payment on delivery works there for
orders up to 10,000 ₴ (KB-046).

## Collecting your parcel

- How long the parcel waits at the post office, and any storage fees, are set by Ukrposhta, not by
  us. Keep an eye on the tracking and Ukrposhta's notices, and collect the parcel soon after it
  arrives.
- If nobody collects the parcel and it comes back to our warehouse, we refund the order total minus
  delivery both ways: the delivery to you and the return delivery to us.
- Check the box at the post office before you take it. If it's damaged, open it there with the staff.
  If a bottle is broken, refuse the parcel and ask the staff for a damage report. We'll send a
  replacement for free (KB-050).

## Sending a return with Ukrposhta

You can also use Ukrposhta to send a return back to us. Write to support first to get a return
number and our address, then send the parcel. You pay for return shipping, unless we made a mistake
with your order (KB-048).

## Examples

- Lattafa Yara (1,290 ₴) with Ukrposhta: delivery is 49 ₴.
- Versace Eros Eau de Toilette 50 ml (2,490 ₴) with Ukrposhta: delivery is free, because the products
  total is over 2,000 ₴.
- You pay at 11:00 on a Monday. The order leaves the warehouse on Monday and usually reaches your post
  office within 3–5 days.
- You'd like to pay in cash when the parcel arrives. That isn't available with Ukrposhta, so choose a
  Nova Poshta branch.

## Edge cases

- The order is the same whichever carrier you pick: same packaging, same free sample on orders from
  1,500 ₴ (KB-086), and free gift wrapping if you ask for it (KB-053).
- With us, Ukrposhta is for addresses in Ukraine only. For parcels abroad, see KB-045.
- You can change the delivery option, for example from Ukrposhta to Nova Poshta, until the order is
  dispatched. Write to support@zoomerfume.example with your order number (KB-041).
