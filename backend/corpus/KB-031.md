## Overview

Delina is the signature rose of Parfums de Marly's women's line, created by Quentin Bisch. It's a
juicy pink rose sharpened with tart lychee and rhubarb, drying down to soft musk: feminine, polished
and a little candy-bright. Several flankers share its name, such as Delina Exclusif and Delina La
Rosée. This card is about the original Eau de Parfum, the only Delina we carry. We have only a few
bottles left. It's one of the niche perfumes we also sell as a 10 ml decant. Our Parfums de Marly
prices were updated on 1 October 2026 (see KB-095).

## Notes

- **Top:** bergamot, lychee, rhubarb
- **Heart:** Damascena rose, nutmeg
- **Base:** cashmeran, musks, vetiver

This is the brand's current list. Fragrance databases give a longer one, with Turkish rose, peony,
vanilla and incense, and put nutmeg in the top.

## How it wears

It opens sparkling and tart: lychee and rhubarb with a squeeze of bergamot, almost like a pink fruit
soda. Then the rose blooms, fresh and dewy rather than powdery, with a warm hint of nutmeg. The
dry-down is soft musk and cashmeran with a touch of vetiver. On our team it lasted 8 hours or more,
with a moderate-to-strong trail that people notice when you walk past.

## Who it's for

- Rose lovers who want a modern, fruity rose rather than a classic powdery one.
- Weddings, dates, celebrations and other special days.
- Spring and autumn, and mild winter days.

Who it's not for: people who don't like rose, or who want a warm, cozy scent. For a creamy vanilla
instead, see Ariana Grande Cloud Eau de Parfum (KB-022).

## Zoomer's take
