## Short answer

Your parcel waits at the Nova Poshta branch or parcel locker for 5 days free of charge. After that,
Nova Poshta charges a daily storage fee. If nobody collects the parcel within 14 days, it's returned to
our warehouse.

## An example

Your parcel arrives at the branch on a Monday. You can collect it free of charge until the end of
Friday. From Saturday, Nova Poshta adds its storage fee for every extra day. If the parcel is still
there two weeks after it arrived, it's returned to our warehouse.

Parcel lockers follow the same 5-day rule. The locker code in your SMS stops working once the parcel
is returned.

## After the first 5 days

The storage fee is set by Nova Poshta, not by us, and you pay it when you collect the parcel. The
carrier's app and SMS reminders show the last free day, so keep an eye on them.

## If the parcel is returned to our warehouse

- **Prepaid order:** we refund the order total minus delivery both ways: the delivery to you and the
  return delivery to us.
- **Cash on delivery:** there's nothing to refund, because you haven't paid yet.

Either way, the order is closed. If you still want the perfume, place a new order.

## How to avoid it

- Your tracking number arrives by SMS and email as soon as the parcel leaves our warehouse (see
  KB-072). Most parcels reach the branch in 1–2 days.
- If you'll be away, choose a delivery you can time: the Nova Poshta courier to your door, or our
  same-day courier if you're in Kyiv (see KB-042 and KB-043).
- Ordering a gift for someone else? Make sure they know a parcel is coming.
