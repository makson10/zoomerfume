## Summary

If your address is in Kyiv, our own courier can bring your order on the day you place it, between
17:00 and 21:00. Place the order by 13:00, Monday to Saturday. Delivery costs 199 ₴ and is free from
5,000 ₴. You pay online at checkout or by card to the courier. The service started on 7 April 2026
(KB-091) and works only within Kyiv.

## How it works

1. At checkout, choose the same-day courier and enter your address in Kyiv.
2. Place the order by 13:00 and pay online, or choose to pay the courier by card.
3. We pack your order at our Kyiv warehouse (KB-062), and the courier sets off with it the same day.
4. The courier brings the parcel to your address between 17:00 and 21:00.
5. Check the parcel in front of the courier before they leave (KB-050).

## When to order

| You order | Delivered |
|---|---|
| Monday–Saturday, by 13:00 | the same day, 17:00–21:00 |
| Monday–Friday, after 13:00 | the next day, 17:00–21:00 |
| Saturday after 13:00, or Sunday | Monday, 17:00–21:00 |

The courier works from Monday to Saturday. Its 13:00 cut-off is earlier than the 15:00 cut-off for
Nova Poshta and Ukrposhta parcels (KB-041).

## Price

- Delivery costs 199 ₴.
- It's free when your products total reaches 5,000 ₴. The total is counted after discounts, Zoomer
  Points and gift cards.
- The free delivery of the Icon tier is for Nova Poshta (KB-056). With the Kyiv courier, Icon members
  pay 199 ₴ below 5,000 ₴, like everyone else.

## Paying

You have two options:

- **Online at checkout:** card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, or pay in parts for orders from 3,000 ₴
  (KB-047). Gift cards and points can cover part of the order (KB-046).
- **By card to the courier:** the courier brings a card terminal, and you pay when the parcel
  arrives. The courier doesn't accept cash. There's no extra fee for paying this way.

Sending a perfume to someone in Kyiv as a gift? Pay online, so the recipient pays nothing at the door
(KB-080). Gift wrapping is free (KB-053).

## Where we deliver

The courier delivers to addresses within the city of Kyiv. Towns and villages outside the city aren't
covered. If you live outside Kyiv, Nova Poshta's courier brings parcels to your door in 1–2 days
(KB-042).

## Examples

- You order Carolina Herrera Good Girl 50 ml (4,090 ₴) at 11:30 on a Tuesday and pay online. The
  courier brings it on Tuesday between 17:00 and 21:00. Delivery is 199 ₴.
- You order Tom Ford Tobacco Vanille 30 ml (7,990 ₴) at 12:45 on a Saturday and pay the courier by
  card. It arrives the same Saturday, and delivery is free.
- You order at 14:10 on a Wednesday. The courier brings it on Thursday between 17:00 and 21:00.
- Your address is in a town just outside Kyiv. The same-day courier isn't available there, so choose
  Nova Poshta.

## Edge cases

- Pre-order products ship only when the stock arrives, usually in 2–4 weeks, so they can't come the
  same day (KB-054).
- Make sure someone can take the parcel between 17:00 and 21:00. If your plans change, write to
  support@zoomerfume.example as soon as you can, with your order number.
- Changes and cancellation follow the usual rule: you can make them until the parcel leaves the
  warehouse (KB-041).
- Damaged box or wrong item? Open the box with the courier, or write to support within 3 days if you
  find a problem later (KB-050).
