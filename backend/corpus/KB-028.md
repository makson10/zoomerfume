## Overview

Aventus is the fragrance that made the house of Creed famous far beyond perfume fans. Launched in
2010, it pairs a juicy pineapple and citrus opening with a dry, smoky birch base, and it has become a
reference point for confident men's scents. It's one of the niche perfumes we also sell as a 10 ml
decant. Our Creed prices were updated on 1 October 2026 (see KB-095).

## Notes

Creed rewrote its official note list in recent years. This is the current one:

- **Top:** Calabrian bergamot, Sicilian lemon, blackcurrant leaf
- **Heart:** pineapple, pink pepper, jasmine
- **Base:** birch, patchouli, musk

Older lists that you may still see elsewhere also mention apple, oakmoss and ambroxan.

## How it wears

It opens bright and fruity: pineapple, lemon and a green snap of blackcurrant leaf. After about an
hour the smoky birch shows up, and the scent turns drier and more serious before it settles into clean
musk. On our team it lasted 7–10 hours, with a noticeable trail for the first few.

Aventus is known for small differences between production batches, so two bottles can feel slightly
different in strength. That's normal for this perfume and not a sign of a fake. Every bottle we sell
comes from an authorized source (see KB-051).

## Who it's for

- Someone who wants one signature scent for work, dates and weekends.
- Fans of fresh, fruity scents who find most "fresh" perfumes too generic.
- Spring to autumn. On a hot day, two sprays are plenty.

Who it's not for: people who want something sweet or cozy, and anyone on a tight budget. For the
second group, see Club de Nuit Intense Man below.

## Zoomer's take

The main character of men's niche perfume, and honestly the hype is mostly earned. It's pricey, so
start with the 10 ml decant: that's about a month of daily wear. If you fall for it, the 100 ml bottle
works out cheaper per ml than the 50 ml.
