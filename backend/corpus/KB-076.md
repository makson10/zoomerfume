## Short answer

Sign in to your account before checkout, then choose how many Zoomer Points to use. You can spend
points once you have at least 100, and they can pay for up to 30% of the products total. 1 point is
worth 1 ₴.

## Step by step

1. Sign in with your phone number. Points live in your account, so a guest checkout can't use them.
2. Add your products to the cart and go to checkout.
3. Enter how many points you'd like to use, up to the limit for this order.
4. Pay the rest with any payment method that's available for your order.

## An example

Your products come to 5,000 ₴. The 30% limit means points can cover up to 1,500 ₴ of that. If you
have more points than you can use, the rest stay in your account for your next order. You pay the
remaining amount by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or another method you choose.

If you have fewer than 100 points, you can't use them yet. Keep collecting, and they'll be ready on
a later order.

## Good to know

- **Points from this order come later.** Points arrive 14 days after delivery, so you can't spend
  the points from an order on that same order.
- **The part paid with points earns nothing.** You earn points on the rest of the products total.
- **Free delivery is counted after points.** Nova Poshta branch and Ukrposhta delivery are free from
  2,000 ₴, and that total is checked after your points. If points bring your products total under
  2,000 ₴, you pay for delivery as usual.
- **Points combine with a promo code and a gift card** in the same order (see KB-084).
- **Returns:** points you used on a product you return come back to your account.
- **Points aren't cash.** They can only be spent on products in our shop.

## Can Zoomer apply my points?

No. Zoomer can't see accounts or orders, so it can't check your balance or use points for you.
Your account page shows your points. For a question about points on a specific order, write to
support@zoomerfume.example with your order number.
