## Short answer

You pay the full price at checkout, and we ship your order as soon as the stock reaches us, usually
in 2–4 weeks. You can cancel any time before dispatch and get a full refund. Products that work this
way are marked "pre-order" on their cards.

## Step by step

1. Find a perfume marked "pre-order". Right now that includes Prada Paradoxe (KB-018) and Creed
   Silver Mountain Water (KB-029).
2. Order it and pay the full price at checkout.
3. When the stock arrives, your order leaves our Kyiv warehouse, and you get the tracking number by
   SMS and email.
4. Delivery then takes the usual time for the option you chose, for example 1–2 days with Nova
   Poshta.

## Paying for a pre-order

A pre-order is paid in full when you place it, so cash on delivery isn't available for pre-orders.
The price you pay at checkout is the price of your order, even if the stock takes a few weeks to
arrive.

## Changing your mind

Write to support@zoomerfume.example with your order number any time before the order is dispatched,
and we refund the full amount. Waiting longer than you'd like is a perfectly good reason. Zoomer, our
AI helper, can't see or cancel orders, so please write to support directly.

Once the order has been dispatched, the usual returns rules apply: unopened, with the cellophane
intact, within 14 days of receipt (see KB-048).

## Zoomer Points

Points for a pre-order work like on any other order. They're counted on what you paid for the
products and arrive 14 days after delivery.

## An example

You pre-order Silver Mountain Water and pay at checkout. A few weeks later the stock arrives, your
order is dispatched, and the tracking number comes by SMS and email. A day or two later the parcel is
at your Nova Poshta branch. If you had changed your mind before dispatch, one email to support would
have got you a full refund.
