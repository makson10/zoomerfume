## Overview

Mugler, formerly known as Thierry Mugler, launched Alien in 2005. It was created by Dominique Ropion
and Laurent Bruyère. The formula is deliberately simple: a radiant jasmine, a warm cashmere wood and a
glowing white amber. The result is bold, sleek and instantly recognizable, and it comes in the
amethyst-colored "talisman" bottle. Alien Goddess and Alien Hypersense are separate perfumes, not
stronger versions of this one. This card is for the original Alien Eau de Parfum.

## Notes

- **Top:** jasmine sambac
- **Heart:** cashmeran (cashmere wood)
- **Base:** white amber

Mugler's own pages don't always agree on whether the cashmere wood sits in the heart or the base.

## How it wears

Jasmine comes first, bright and almost sunny, with a slightly sweet, waxy feel. Within an hour the
woody warmth moves in, and then the white amber glows for the rest of the day. It's one of the
longest-lasting perfumes in our shop: on our team it easily lasted 10 hours or more, with a strong
trail for most of that time. One or two sprays are enough, even in winter. Because it changes so
little over the day, people tend to either fall for it straight away or know within minutes that it
isn't for them.

## Who it's for

- Someone who wants a signature scent that people remember.
- Evenings, formal events and nights out in autumn and winter.
- Jasmine lovers who want it bold and warm, not delicate.

Who it's not for: close office spaces, or anyone who prefers a quiet scent. For a softer jasmine, try
Valentino Born in Roma Donna (KB-017).

## Zoomer's take

Alien doesn't do subtle, and that's the whole point. Glowy, warm, a bit mysterious, and people will
remember you walked in. Seriously, one or two sprays. Three is a whole weather system. If you're
curious but nervous, a sample first is the move.
