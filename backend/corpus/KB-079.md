## Short answer

Yes, with Nova Poshta. You can pay in cash or by card when you collect the parcel, for orders up to
10,000 ₴. Nova Poshta charges its own fee for this, 20 ₴ plus 2% of the amount, and you pay it. In
Kyiv, our same-day courier also takes card payment at the door.

## Which delivery options allow it

| Delivery | Pay on delivery? |
|---|---|
| Nova Poshta branch | Yes, cash or card |
| Nova Poshta parcel locker | No, prepaid only |
| Nova Poshta courier to the door | Yes, cash or card |
| Our same-day courier in Kyiv | Yes, by card to the courier |
| Ukrposhta | No, prepaid only |
| International | No, prepaid only |

## The Nova Poshta fee

The cash-on-delivery fee is set by Nova Poshta, not by us, and you pay it when you collect the
parcel. Say your products come to 2,000 ₴. Branch delivery is free from that amount, so the parcel
costs 2,000 ₴. Nova Poshta's fee is 20 ₴ plus 2% of 2,000 ₴, which makes 60 ₴ in total. If you'd
rather skip the fee, pay online by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.

## Before you choose it

- **Orders over 10,000 ₴** can't be paid on delivery with Nova Poshta. Pay online instead, or use
  pay in parts, which works for orders from 3,000 ₴ (see KB-078).
- **Dispatch:** a cash-on-delivery order leaves the warehouse the same day if it's confirmed by 15:00,
  Monday to Friday.
- **Refusing the parcel:** you can refuse a cash-on-delivery parcel at the branch. After two refusals
  without a reason, your next order needs prepayment.
- **Sending a gift?** Choose prepayment so the recipient doesn't pay anything (see KB-080).
- **Returns:** for a cash-on-delivery order, the refund goes to a bank card you give us in your
  return email (see KB-048).

## Check before you pay

At a Nova Poshta branch, you can look at the parcel before you take it. If the box is damaged, open
it with the staff. If a bottle is broken, refuse the parcel and ask for a damage report, and we'll
send a replacement for free (see KB-071).
