## What's in your parcel

Every order travels in the same simple setup:

- **A recycled cardboard box.** We pick a box size that fits the order, so there's less empty space.
- **Paper fill** around the bottle, so it can't move during delivery.
- **Paper tape** to seal the box.
- **No plastic bubble wrap.** Paper fill protects a perfume box just as well, and it's much easier to
  get rid of.

Our boxes are plain on purpose. They protect the perfume, and that's all they need to do.

## The cellophane on your perfume

The one wrapping we never remove is the factory cellophane on the perfume's own box. It comes from the
brand, and we leave it on for two reasons: it shows the perfume hasn't been opened, and it has to stay
intact if you want to return an unopened product (KB-048).

## Gift boxes

Gift wrapping is free. A gift order goes into our grape-and-pink gift box with a card and your message.
There's never a price inside a gift parcel (KB-053).

## After you unpack

The box, the paper fill and the paper tape are all paper or cardboard. You can put them straight in
with your paper waste, with no plastic to pick out first.

## Less waste starts before you order

The most wasteful purchase is a perfume you never wear. That's why we're so keen on samples:

- A 2 ml sample lets you wear a perfume for a few days before you decide on a full bottle (KB-052).
- Niche perfumes also come as 10 ml decants, a good size if you want a scent for a holiday or a month
  instead of a big bottle.
- Every order from 1,500 ₴ comes with a free sample, so you can discover something new without
  ordering extra.

Our product cards also say who each perfume is *not* for (KB-060). An honest "skip this one" saves you
money, and it means one less perfume you'll never wear.

## If something arrives damaged

Paper fill keeps perfume boxes well protected, but a carrier can still have a bad day. If the box looks
damaged when you collect it, open it at the branch with the staff. The steps are in KB-050, and we send
a replacement for free.
