## Overview

Yara is Lattafa's soft, sweet women's perfume, launched in 2020 and easy to spot in its pink bottle.
It blends tropical fruit and powdery heliotrope with a milky vanilla and musk base, and it's one of
the most talked-about affordable perfumes online. Lattafa has released many flankers with "Yara" in
the name, and this card is for the original. At 1,290 ₴, it's the most affordable perfume in our
catalog. Its sibling in our shop is Lattafa Khamrah (KB-037), which is darker and spicier.

## Notes

- **Top:** orchid, heliotrope, tangerine
- **Heart:** gourmand accord, tropical fruits
- **Base:** vanilla, musk, sandalwood

## How it wears

It opens with juicy tangerine and a soft, powdery heliotrope that feels a bit like almond cream. The
heart is creamy and fruity, like a tropical milkshake. The dry-down is a gentle vanilla musk with a
hint of sandalwood. It's soft rather than loud, and it never turns sharp or sticky.

On our team it lasted 5–7 hours. It has a light trail for the first couple of hours, then stays
fairly close to the skin. Some shops describe it as much stronger than that, but in our experience
it's a cozy, personal scent.

## Who it's for

- Fans of sweet, creamy scents who want something cuddly, not bold.
- Everyday wear all year round: classes, work, weekends and casual plans.
- A first perfume, or an easy gift that's hard to get wrong.

Who it's not for: anyone looking for a fresh or floral scent, or for big projection. For a light,
fresh option, try Marc Jacobs Daisy Eau de Toilette (KB-020).

## Zoomer's take

A pink milkshake in a bottle, and the cheapest full bottle in our shop. It won't fill a room, but
the people who hug you will ask what you're wearing. It works all year, though it feels best in
spring and autumn. If you like it soft and sweet, this is your sign. Not sure it's too sweet
for you? A 69 ₴ sample answers that in one day.
