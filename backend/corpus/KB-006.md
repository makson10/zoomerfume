## Overview

Lady Million is the women's scent of the Million family, launched in 2010 as the partner to the
men's 1 Million. It's a bright, glamorous floral: raspberry and neroli on top, a big bouquet of
white flowers in the middle and honey in the base. The house has been called Rabanne since 2023
(formerly Paco Rabanne), and many shops still list it under the old name. It shares the gold look
with the men's 1 Million Eau de Toilette (KB-004) and Parfum (KB-005), but not the notes. Lady
Million has many flankers. This card is the original Eau de Parfum.

## Notes

- **Top:** raspberry, neroli
- **Heart:** jasmine, orange blossom, gardenia
- **Base:** honey, patchouli

Longer lists you may see elsewhere also mention lemon in the top and amber in the base.

## How it wears

The opening is juicy raspberry with a sparkle of citrus. Then the white flowers bloom, and the honey
makes everything sweet and golden. The patchouli keeps the dry-down from getting too sugary. On most
of our team it lasted 6–10 hours, with a strong trail for the first few hours. It's not a quiet
perfume, so one or two sprays are enough for an evening out. The raspberry fades first, and by late
evening it's mostly honey and soft patchouli.

## Who it's for

- Someone who loves sweet, sparkling florals and doesn't mind being noticed.
- Evenings, parties, birthdays and celebrations, mostly in autumn and winter.
- A gift for someone who likes glamorous, statement scents.

Who it's not for: anyone looking for something light and fresh for daytime. For that, try Marc
Jacobs Daisy Eau de Toilette (KB-020).
