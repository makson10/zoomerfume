## Short answer

Yes, for orders from 3,000 ₴. You can split the payment into 3 interest-free monthly payments
through one of our partner banks. It's approved instantly at checkout, and you'll need a card from a
partner bank.

## How it works

1. Add your products to the cart and go to checkout.
2. Choose pay in parts as your payment method. It's available once your order reaches 3,000 ₴, and
   the partner banks are shown right there.
3. Confirm with your partner bank card. The bank approves it straight away.
4. Your order is dispatched like any other paid order. Paid by 15:00 Monday to Friday, it leaves our
   warehouse the same day.

## An example

Your order comes to 3,000 ₴. With pay in parts, you pay 1,000 ₴ a month for three months, with no
interest. You get the perfume now and spread the cost over the next few months.

## Good to know

- **Under 3,000 ₴?** Pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, or pay in cash or by card when you
  collect the parcel at Nova Poshta (see KB-079).
- **No partner bank card?** Then pay in parts isn't available, and you can choose another payment
  method (see KB-046).
- **Not approved?** You can still pay for the order in full in one of the other ways.
- **Returns:** if you return the order, the partner bank cancels the remaining payments and gives
  back what you've already paid (see KB-048).

## Questions about your payment

Zoomer, our AI helper, can explain how pay in parts works, but it can't see payments, bank approvals
or orders. For a question about one specific order, write to support@zoomerfume.example with your
order number. Our support team works every day from 9:00 to 21:00 Kyiv time. For anything about the
monthly payments themselves, your partner bank is the place to ask.
