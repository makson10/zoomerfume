## Overview

Wood Sage & Sea Salt is Jo Malone London's take on a walk along a windy, rocky coast. It launched in
2014 and was created by Christine Nagel. Instead of the usual sweet or citrusy freshness, it goes for
something drier: salt, mineral air and soft herbal sage. It's unisex and quiet by design, and it's a
Cologne, the lightest strength Jo Malone makes. Our team reaches for it on days when they want to smell
fresh without smelling "perfumed". Read the wear notes below before you pick a size.

## Notes

- **Top:** ambrette seeds
- **Heart:** sea salt
- **Base:** sage

Jo Malone keeps its official list short. Some fragrance databases also mention grapefruit, seaweed
and mineral notes.

## How it wears

The opening is bright and gently musky from the ambrette. Then the salty, mineral heart takes over:
think damp stones, sea spray and a breeze, not a sweet "ocean" scent. The sage keeps it dry and herbal
to the end. To be honest, this one is soft. On our team it lasted 2–4 hours and stayed close to the
body the whole time. That's the most common complaint about it, and we'd rather you know before you
buy a big bottle.

## Who it's for

- People who want to smell fresh and clean, not "perfumed", at the office or on a beach day.
- Anyone who finds most fresh scents too sweet, too sporty or too loud.
- Spring and summer: daytime, casual weekends and trips.

Who it's not for: anyone who wants a scent that lasts all day or leaves a trail. For a fresh scent with
much more power, see Rabanne Invictus Eau de Toilette (KB-007).

## Zoomer's take

It's the "just got back from the seaside and didn't even try" scent. Super chill, super wearable, and
nobody will ever say it's too much. The catch: it's shy and fades fast, so plan to top it up during the
day. Start with the 30 ml and see if you love it enough to keep it on your desk.
