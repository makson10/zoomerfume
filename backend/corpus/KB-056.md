## Summary

Zoomer Points has three tiers: Fresh, Hype and Icon. Your tier depends on the total of your orders in
the last 12 months, and we recalculate it on the 1st of every month. Higher tiers earn more points:
5% for Fresh, 7% for Hype and 10% for Icon. Icon members also get free Nova Poshta delivery on every
order and early access to sales.

## The three tiers

| Tier | Your orders in the last 12 months | Points you earn | Extras |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fresh | from 0 ₴ | 5% | — |
| Hype | from 10,000 ₴ | 7% | — |
| Icon | from 30,000 ₴ | 10% | free Nova Poshta delivery on every order, early access to sales |

Everyone starts in Fresh when they create an account. The earn rate is a percentage of what you pay
for products, after discounts (see KB-055).

## How your tier is set

1. On the 1st of each month, we add up your orders from the last 12 months.
2. If the total has passed a threshold, you move up. If it has dropped below the threshold of your
   current tier, you move down. Otherwise you stay where you are.
3. The new tier applies for the whole month, until the next recalculation.

A few details:

- Passing a threshold in the middle of a month doesn't change your tier straight away. The new tier
  starts on the 1st of the next month.
- Each order earns points at the rate of the tier you're in on the day you place it.
- Products you return don't count toward your 12-month total.
- Your current tier is shown on your account page.
- A change of tier never touches points you already have. They keep their expiry dates (see KB-055).

## What Icon members get

- **Free Nova Poshta delivery on every order,** whatever the order total. If you pay cash on
  delivery, Nova Poshta still charges its own fee for that (see KB-046).
- **Early access to sales.** For Black Friday 2026, Icon members can start shopping on 26 November
  2026 at 18:00, before the sale opens for everyone on 27 November (see KB-057).
- **10% back in points** on every order.

International delivery isn't part of the Icon deal. It costs 590 ₴ for every order (see KB-045).

## Examples

- **One bottle, three tiers.** Versace Eros Eau de Toilette 50 ml (KB-010) costs 2,490 ₴. It earns
  124 points in Fresh, 174 points in Hype and 249 points in Icon.
- **Moving up.** In early March 2026, your orders over the last 12 months add up to a little under
  10,000 ₴. On 14 March you buy Yves Saint Laurent Libre 50 ml for 3,690 ₴, and your total passes
  10,000 ₴. You're still in Fresh for the rest of March, so that order earns 184 points (5%). On
  1 April you become Hype, and your orders from then on earn 7%.
- **Moving down.** In August 2025 you bought two large niche bottles in one order. Together with your
  other orders, that took your total over 30,000 ₴, and you became Icon on 1 September 2025. Over the
  next year you bought a few more bottles, worth more than 10,000 ₴ in total. On 1 September 2026,
  the August 2025 order dropped out of the 12-month window. Your total was now between 10,000 ₴ and
  30,000 ₴, so you moved to Hype. All the points you had earned stayed in your account.
- **Staying put.** You're in Hype. On 1 May your total for the last 12 months is still over
  10,000 ₴ but under 30,000 ₴, so you stay in Hype for May.

## Edge cases

- **Guest orders** don't count toward a tier, because points and tiers need an account (see KB-083).
- **Gift cards** are not products, so buying one doesn't earn points at any tier (see KB-053).
