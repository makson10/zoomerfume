## Short answer

First check the tracking number we sent by SMS and email when the parcel left our warehouse.
Delivery times count from dispatch, not from the moment you ordered: 1–2 days with Nova Poshta, 3–5
days with Ukrposhta and 5–10 days abroad. If the parcel is past that time or the tracking hasn't
moved, write to support with your order number.

## Count from dispatch

Orders paid by 15:00 Monday to Friday leave the same day. Later orders leave the next working day,
weekend orders leave on Monday, and public holidays move dispatch to the next working day.

For example, you order on Saturday. The parcel leaves on Monday, and with Nova Poshta it usually
reaches your branch on Tuesday or Wednesday. It isn't late on Monday evening, even though you
ordered two days earlier.

## What the tracking tells you

- **The parcel has arrived.** It may already be waiting at your branch or parcel locker. A Nova
  Poshta parcel waits 5 days free of charge (see KB-075).
- **The parcel hasn't moved for a while.** Write to us, and we'll check with the carrier.
- **There's no tracking number at all.** The order may not have left yet. See KB-072.

## Kyiv courier and international parcels

- Our same-day courier in Kyiv delivers between 17:00 and 21:00 for orders placed by 13:00, Monday
  to Saturday. If nobody has come by 21:00, write to us.
- International parcels go with Nova Post by ground and take 5–10 days. Use the Nova Post tracking
  number to see where the parcel is.

## When to write to us

Zoomer, our AI helper, can't see orders, so it can't check where a parcel is. For anything about
one specific order, write to support@zoomerfume.example with your order number. Our team is there
every day from 9:00 to 21:00 Kyiv time and usually replies within 2 hours. We'll check the parcel
with the carrier and tell you what happens next.

If the parcel finally arrives damaged, check it at the branch before you take it (see KB-071).
