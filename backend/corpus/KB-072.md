## Short answer

As soon as your parcel leaves our warehouse, you get the carrier's tracking number by SMS and email.
Use it with the carrier to follow the parcel. Before dispatch there's no tracking number yet, but
your order confirmation by email and SMS shows that we've received the order.

## When the tracking number arrives

- Orders paid by 15:00 Monday to Friday leave the warehouse the same day. For cash on delivery, the
  order needs to be confirmed by 15:00.
- Orders after 15:00 leave the next working day.
- Saturday and Sunday orders leave on Monday.
- Public holidays move dispatch to the next working day.

The tracking number comes on the day the parcel leaves.

## Which carrier tracks it

The tracking number belongs to the carrier you chose at checkout: Nova Poshta, Ukrposhta, or Nova
Post for international orders. If you chose our same-day courier in Kyiv, the parcel comes to you
the same evening, between 17:00 and 21:00.

## An example

You pay for an order on Friday at 16:30. It leaves the warehouse on Monday, and the tracking number
reaches you by SMS and email that day. With Nova Poshta, the parcel usually reaches your branch on
Tuesday or Wednesday. The carrier then lets you know it has arrived. Remember that it waits there
for 5 days free of charge (see KB-075).

## No tracking number?

- Check your spam folder for our email.
- Check when you paid against the dispatch times above. An order paid on Friday evening won't have
  a tracking number until Monday.
- Still nothing after the dispatch day? Write to us.

## Questions about your order

Zoomer, our AI helper, can't see orders, so it can't look up your parcel or its status. For anything
about one specific order, write to support@zoomerfume.example with your order number. Our support
team works every day from 9:00 to 21:00 Kyiv time and usually replies within 2 hours. Messages sent
later get a reply the next morning.
