## Summary

Check your parcel when you collect it. If the box is damaged, open it there with the staff. If a
bottle is broken, refuse the parcel and ask the carrier for a damage report, and we'll send a
replacement for free. If you find a defect or a wrong item at home, write to support within 3 days of
receiving the parcel, with photos or a video. We pay shipping both ways and replace the product or
refund it.

## At the branch: check before you take it

1. Look at the box before you take the parcel, at the Nova Poshta branch or the Ukrposhta post office.
2. If the box is dented, torn, wet or smells strongly of perfume, ask the staff to open it with you
   there.
3. If a bottle inside is broken, refuse the parcel and ask the carrier's staff for a damage report.
4. Write to support@zoomerfume.example with your order number and a photo of the damage report.
5. We send you a replacement for free. You don't pay for the new delivery. If that perfume is no
   longer in stock, we refund it instead.

If the box looks fine and the bottle is intact, take the parcel as usual.

## With a courier

If a courier brings the parcel, the Nova Poshta courier or our same-day courier in Kyiv (KB-043),
check the box in front of them. If it's damaged, open it together before the courier leaves, and
follow the same steps. If you pay on delivery, don't pay for a parcel with a broken bottle: refuse
it instead.

## Found a problem at home

Some problems only show up after you've opened the parcel:

- **A defect:** the sprayer doesn't work, or the bottle leaks.
- **A wrong item:** a different perfume or a different size from the one you ordered.
- **Damage you couldn't see at pickup**, for example in a parcel collected from a locker.

What to do:

1. Write to support within 3 days of receiving the parcel. Include your order number and photos or a
   short video that shows the problem.
2. Support replies with the next steps and a return number. During support hours (9:00–21:00 every
   day) we usually reply within 2 hours (KB-063).
3. Send the product back to us. We pay the shipping both ways.
4. We send a replacement or refund the product.

A defective bottle can be returned even after you've opened it. That's the one exception to the rule
that opened perfume can't be returned (KB-048).

## Refunds

- Refunds go back the way you paid, within 7 working days after we confirm the return. For cash on
  delivery, the money goes to a bank card you give us in your email (KB-048).
- If the whole order was our mistake, for example every product in it is wrong, we also refund the
  original delivery.

## What isn't a defect

- You don't like the scent, or it wears differently on your skin than you expected. That's a regular
  return, and only unopened products can go back (KB-048, KB-070).
- You have doubts about whether a bottle is original. Write to support with the batch code from the
  box, and we send the distributor's certificate (KB-051).

## Cash on delivery and refusals

If you refuse a cash-on-delivery parcel because it's damaged, that's a refusal with a reason. It
doesn't count toward the rule that two refusals without a reason mean your next order must be
prepaid (KB-041).

## Examples

- At the branch, the box is wet and smells of perfume. You open it with the staff, and a bottle is
  broken. You refuse the parcel, get a damage report, and email support. We send a new bottle for
  free.
- You open a sealed bottle at home and the sprayer doesn't work. You email support the next day with a
  short video. We pay to have it sent back and send you a working bottle.
- You ordered the 50 ml size and got 100 ml. You write to support within 3 days with a photo. We
  send you the 50 ml bottle and pay the shipping both ways.

## Edge cases

- The 3 days start on the day you collected the parcel, as shown in the carrier's tracking.
- Keep the outer box and the packaging until the issue is settled. Support may ask for photos of it.
