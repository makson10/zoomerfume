## Overview

Cloud is the 2018 perfume from Ariana Grande's fragrance line, created by Clément Gavarry. It's a
soft, creamy gourmand: whipped coconut and praline with a light lavender-and-pear lift on top. It won a
Fragrance Foundation award in 2019 and is now one of the best-known affordable sweet scents in the
world. It's also one of the most affordable perfumes in our catalog. If you loved Kayali Vanilla 28,
which we no longer sell (KB-023), Cloud is one of the scents we suggest instead.

## Notes

- **Top:** lavender blossom, pear, bergamot
- **Heart:** whipped coconut cream, praline, vanilla orchid
- **Base:** musks, creamy woods

Some fragrance databases split the heart into two separate notes, whipped cream and coconut.

## How it wears

The first spray is airy and a little fruity, with juicy pear and a soft touch of lavender that keeps
the sweetness from feeling heavy. Within the hour it turns into what most people know it for: a fluffy
blend of coconut cream and praline, warm but never sticky. The dry-down is clean musk with a creamy,
woody softness. On most of our team it lasted a full working day. The trail is moderate at first, then
it settles into a cozy, creamy scent close to the body.

## Who it's for

- Fans of sweet, creamy scents who want something soft rather than loud.
- A first perfume or an easy everyday signature for school, university or work.
- All year round: cozy in winter, and still light enough for a warm spring or summer day.

Who it's not for: anyone who wants a fresh, clean or "barely there" scent, because Cloud stays sweet
from start to finish. For something light and bright, try Marc Jacobs Daisy (KB-020).

## Zoomer's take

Literally a hug in a hoodie, but make it perfume. Sweet, creamy, a bit dreamy, and the price is very
friendly. People love to call it a budget Baccarat Rouge 540. That's a stretch, but they do share the
same soft, glowy mood. The 100 ml bottle is the best deal per ml, so if you already know you love it,
go big.
