## Overview

We no longer sell this perfume (since 16 June 2026). This page stays up so you can still read about it.

Our authorized supplier stopped delivering Vanilla 28 to Ukraine. We only sell bottles that come from
authorized sources (see KB-051), so we didn't look for it anywhere else, and we have no bottles or
samples left. The news post about it is KB-092.

Vanilla 28 launched in 2018 and was composed by Gabriela Chelariu. The brand writes its name as
"Vanilla | 28". It's a dense, ambery vanilla with brown-sugar warmth, darker and less candy-like than
most vanilla perfumes. If you came here looking for it, the last section lists three scents in our
shop with a similar warm, sweet mood.

## Notes

- **Top:** Madagascan vanilla orchid, creamy jasmine
- **Heart:** vanilla infusion, vanilla surabsolute, tonka bean
- **Base:** brown sugar, amber woods, musk, patchouli

Some fragrance databases move the brown sugar and tonka into the heart.

## How it wears

It opens with creamy vanilla and a soft touch of jasmine, then quickly gets deeper and warmer as the
tonka and brown sugar come in. The dry-down is ambery woods with a slightly boozy, earthy patchouli
edge, so it never feels like pure frosting. When we still sold it, it lasted most of the day on our
team, with a moderate-to-strong trail. Some people found it much quieter on their own skin.

## Who it's for

- Vanilla lovers who want a grown-up, warm vanilla rather than a sugary one.
- Autumn and winter: cozy days, evenings and dates.
- Anyone who wants one rich signature scent for the cold months.

Who it's not for: people who want a fresh or light perfume, or something for hot weather. For that,
look at Marc Jacobs Daisy (KB-020).
