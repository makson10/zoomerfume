## Overview

Invictus Eau de Toilette is Rabanne's fresh, sporty men's scent in the silver trophy-shaped bottle.
It came out in 2013 and was created by four perfumers: Véronique Nyberg, Anne Flipo, Olivier Polge
and Dominique Ropion. The house has been called Rabanne since 2023 (formerly Paco Rabanne). Invictus
has many flankers, such as Intense, Aqua, Legend, Victory and Platinum. This card is the original
Eau de Toilette, and it's the only Invictus we carry. It's often compared with Dior Sauvage Eau de
Toilette (KB-001) and Versace Eros (KB-010) as an everyday crowd-pleaser, and it's the lightest and
most summery of the three.

## Notes

- **Top:** grapefruit
- **Heart:** marine accord
- **Base:** guaiac wood, patchouli, ambergris

Longer lists you may see elsewhere add mandarin, bay leaf, jasmine and oakmoss.

## How it wears

It starts with big, juicy grapefruit, then moves into a salty-sweet sea accord that some people
compare to bubblegum. The base is light wood with a touch of ambergris. It's loud for the first hour
or two and then settles close to the skin. Rabanne itself promises 4–6 hours, and on our team that
was about right. It's a daytime scent, not an all-day-and-night one.

## Who it's for

- Spring and summer days: classes, weekends, the gym and the beach.
- A first fragrance, or an easy everyday one, for a younger guy.
- Anyone who likes fresh scents with a little sweetness.

Who it's not for: cold evenings, or someone who wants a fresh scent that lasts from morning to
night. For that, look at Dior Sauvage Eau de Toilette (KB-001).

## Zoomer's take

Gym-bag fresh, in a good way. Sweet grapefruit, sea salt and a trophy bottle, with zero effort
needed. Some people say it's a bit 2013, but it's still a crowd-pleaser for a reason. The 100 ml is
3,490 ₴, which is a solid deal for something you'll wear all summer. If you want a scent you can
spray without thinking before a sunny day out, this is it.
