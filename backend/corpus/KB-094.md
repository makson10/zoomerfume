## Five years of Zoomerfume

Zoomerfume turned five this year. It all began in March 2021, when Daryna and Oleh started splitting
their own bottles into decants for friends. Since then we've shipped over 120,000 orders, grown to a
team of 27 and built a catalog of about 40 perfumes. Thank you for every order and every question
along the way.

## The birthday sale

- **What:** −15% on everything with the code ZOOMER5.
- **When:** from 15 to 30 September 2026.
- **How:** enter ZOOMER5 at checkout.

## Good to know

- You can use one promo code per order, so ZOOMER5 can't be combined with another code.
- You can still pay part of the order with Zoomer Points or a gift card on top of the code (KB-057).
- Points are earned on what you pay after the discount (KB-055).
- Free delivery thresholds use the products total after discounts, so check that your order still
  qualifies (KB-042).

## A good moment for that bottle

Been wearing a sample of something for weeks? This is a good time to get the full bottle. If you
bought a sample of the same perfume in the last 30 days, its price also comes back to you as Zoomer
Points (KB-052).

Not sure what to pick? Zoomer can help you narrow it down in the site chat, any time of day (KB-064).

Here's to the next five years.
