## Overview

1 Million Eau de Toilette came out in 2008 and quickly became one of the best-known sweet men's
scents, helped by its gold-ingot bottle. It was created by Christophe Raynaud, Olivier Pescheux and
Michel Girard. The house has been called Rabanne since 2023. Before that it was Paco Rabanne, and
many shops still use the old name. This is the original of the line. We also carry 1 Million Parfum
(KB-005), which looks almost the same but smells very different, and the women's Lady Million
(KB-006).

## Notes

- **Top:** blood mandarin, grapefruit, mint
- **Heart:** cinnamon, rose, spices
- **Base:** leathery amber, woody notes, Indian patchouli

## How it wears

There's a quick flash of mandarin and mint, and then cinnamon takes over. The heart is sweet and
spicy, and the dry-down is a soft, warm amber with a light leather edge. Rabanne promises 6–8 hours
on skin, and that matches our team: 6–8 hours, with a sweet trail that people around you will
notice. On a jacket or a scarf it stays even longer. It's at its best in cold air, where the
sweetness feels cozy rather than heavy.

## Who it's for

- Someone who wants a sweet, spicy, attention-getting scent for parties and nights out.
- Cold weather: autumn and winter evenings.
- Fans of crowd-pleasers on a budget. The 50 ml bottle is 2,690 ₴.

Who it's not for: summer heat, formal offices, or anyone who doesn't like sweet scents. For a
fresher everyday pick from the same house, see Rabanne Invictus Eau de Toilette (KB-007).

## Zoomer's take

Loud, sweet, cinnamon-y and not shy at all. It's basically a party in a gold bar, glitter included,
and that's the whole point. Two sprays and you're set. Five sprays and the whole bus knows you're
going out tonight. Torn between this and the Parfum? Think season: the EDT for winter nights, the
Parfum for summer ones.
