## Short answer

No. You can order on zoomerfume.example as a guest. An account is only needed if you want to collect
and use Zoomer Points, and you sign in with your phone number.

## Ordering as a guest

Add your products, go to checkout, enter your contact and delivery details and pay. Everything works
the same as with an account:

- Your order confirmation comes by email and SMS.
- The tracking number comes by SMS and email as soon as the parcel leaves our warehouse.
- To change or cancel the order before dispatch, write to support@zoomerfume.example with your order
  number.
- Returns follow the usual rules (see KB-048).

## What an account adds

- **Zoomer Points.** You earn points on your orders and can spend them at checkout (see KB-076).
- **A birthday bonus.** 200 bonus points in your birthday month, if your birthday is set in your
  account.
- **Your points in one place.** The account page lists each batch of points with its own expiry
  date.

If you'd like points for an order, sign in before you place it.

## An example

You order a gift for a friend as a guest, without signing in. A month later you decide you'd like to
collect points. Sign in with your phone number before your next checkout, set your birthday in your
account, and that order earns points. They arrive 14 days after delivery.

## What we store

For an account, we store your phone number, name, order history and delivery addresses, plus your
chats with Zoomer, our AI helper. Zoomer chats are kept to answer your follow-up questions and to
improve the service. We never sell your data. Payments are handled by our payment provider, so we
never see your full card number.

If you'd like your account and data deleted, write to support. It's done within 30 days (see
KB-058).

## Questions

Zoomer can't see accounts or orders. For anything about your account or a specific order, write to
support@zoomerfume.example with your order number. Our support team works every day from 9:00 to
21:00 Kyiv time and usually replies within 2 hours.
