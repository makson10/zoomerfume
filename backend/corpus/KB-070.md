## Short answer

No, sorry. Once a perfume has been opened, we can't take it back for hygiene reasons. The one
exception is a defect, like a sprayer that doesn't work or a leaking bottle. Unopened perfume with
its cellophane intact can be returned within 14 days of receiving it.

## What counts as opened

- The factory cellophane has been removed or torn.
- The perfume has been sprayed, even once.

Our gift wrapping doesn't count. You can open the grape-and-pink gift box, as long as the perfume's
own cellophane is untouched.

## If it's defective

Write to support@zoomerfume.example within 3 days of receiving the parcel. Add your order number and
photos or a short video of the problem. We pay shipping both ways and either replace the bottle or
refund it. The full rules are in KB-050.

## Examples

- You sprayed a new perfume twice and it's just not for you. It can't be returned.
- You removed the cellophane and the sprayer doesn't work. That's a defect: write to us within 3
  days.
- The bottle is still sealed ten days after delivery, and you've changed your mind. That's a normal
  return. Write to support with your order number, send the bottle back with Nova Poshta or
  Ukrposhta at your cost, and get the product price back within 7 working days (see KB-048).

## Next time, start small

Not sure about a scent? Try a 2 ml sample first. It costs 69 ₴, or 119 ₴ for niche perfumes.
Samples can't be returned either, but they cost far less than a full bottle you might not wear. If
you then buy the full bottle within 30 days, the sample price comes back as Zoomer Points (see
KB-052).
