## Summary

Some products on our site are marked "pre-order". You can order them now: you pay the full price at
checkout, and we ship your order as soon as the new stock reaches our Kyiv warehouse, usually in 2–4
weeks. Until the parcel leaves the warehouse, you can cancel at any time and get a full refund.

## How a pre-order works

1. The product card shows a "pre-order" label and says the stock usually arrives in 2–4 weeks.
2. You place the order like any other order and pay the full price.
3. We confirm the order by email and SMS.
4. When the stock arrives, we pack your order and send it. The carrier's tracking number comes by
   SMS and email as soon as the parcel leaves (see KB-072).
5. Delivery then takes the usual time for the option you chose, for example 1–2 days with Nova
   Poshta (see KB-042).

## Paying for a pre-order

- You pay the whole amount when you place the order. Cash on delivery isn't available for
  pre-orders.
- Zoomer Points and gift cards can pay for part of a pre-order, the same as for any order (see
  KB-055 and KB-053).
- The usual free-delivery thresholds apply. For example, Nova Poshta delivery to a branch is free
  for orders from 2,000 ₴.
- Points for a pre-order arrive 14 days after delivery, like points for any other order.

## Cancelling or changing a pre-order

- **Cancel before dispatch:** write to support@zoomerfume.example with your order number. You get a
  full refund, back the way you paid.
- **Change it before dispatch:** a different size or another delivery address is fine too. Write to
  support with the order number and what you'd like to change (see KB-073).
- **After dispatch:** the order can't be changed any more. It becomes a regular order, so the usual
  return rules apply: unopened, with the cellophane intact, within 14 days of receiving it (see
  KB-048).

## Products on pre-order now

- Prada Paradoxe Eau de Parfum (KB-018)
- Creed Silver Mountain Water (KB-029), including its 10 ml decant

Each card shows the sizes and prices. Once the new stock is on our shelves, the "pre-order" label
comes off and the product ships the usual way, the same day if you order by 15:00 on a working day
(see KB-041).

## Examples

- You order Prada Paradoxe 50 ml and pay 4,690 ₴ at checkout. Delivery to your Nova Poshta branch is
  free, because the order is over 2,000 ₴. When the stock arrives, we send the parcel and you get the
  tracking number by SMS and email.
- Ten days after ordering Silver Mountain Water, you change your mind. The parcel hasn't left our
  warehouse, so you write to support and get your money back in full.
- You ordered Prada Paradoxe 30 ml and decide you'd prefer 90 ml. It hasn't shipped, so you
  write to support with your order number and ask to change the size.
- You'd like a sample of Silver Mountain Water first. Samples are only made from perfume we have in
  stock, so it'll be possible once the new stock arrives (see KB-052).

## Edge cases

- **One order, mixed products.** If an order has both a pre-order product and products we already
  have, the whole order ships together, when everything is in. To get the other products sooner,
  place them as a separate order.
- **The wait is longer than you'd like.** You can cancel for a full refund at any time before
  dispatch, even after weeks of waiting.
- **A gift.** Gift wrapping works for pre-orders too (see KB-053). Just keep in mind that the parcel
  ships when the stock arrives.
