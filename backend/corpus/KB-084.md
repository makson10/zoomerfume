## Short answer

Yes. A gift card works like money, so you can use it together with Zoomer Points and a promo code in
the same order. The usual limits still apply: one promo code per order, and points from 100, for up
to 30% of the products total.

## What combines with what

- **Gift card and points:** yes.
- **Gift card and a promo code:** yes.
- **Points and a promo code:** yes.
- **All three at once:** yes.
- **Two promo codes:** no, only one per order.
- **A promo code on sale products:** no. Products that are already on sale don't take promo codes,
  but you can still pay for them with a gift card and points.

## Gift card basics

- Our gift cards are electronic and come in 500, 1,000, 2,000, 3,000 and 5,000 ₴.
- They're valid for 12 months.
- You can spend one over several orders. Whatever you don't use stays on the card for next time.
- They can't be exchanged for cash.
- Buying a gift card doesn't earn points. Paying with one does: the part of an order you pay with a
  gift card earns points as usual.

## An example

You have a 1,000 ₴ gift card, 200 points in your account and a promo code. At checkout you enter the
code, choose to use your 200 points, and apply the gift card to what's left. If the gift card covers
the rest, there's nothing more to pay. If the rest is less than 1,000 ₴, the leftover balance stays
on the card for your next order. If it's more, you pay the difference by card, Apple Pay or Google
Pay.

## Watch the free delivery threshold

Free delivery is counted on the products total after discounts, points and gift cards. Nova Poshta
branch and Ukrposhta delivery are free from 2,000 ₴. So if your promo code, points and gift card
together bring the total under 2,000 ₴, you pay for delivery as usual.

## Questions about your balance

Zoomer, our AI helper, can explain these rules, but it can't see your account, your points or your
gift card. For anything about a specific order, write to support@zoomerfume.example with your order
number, every day from 9:00 to 21:00 Kyiv time.
