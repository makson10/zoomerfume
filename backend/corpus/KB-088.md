## Say hi to Zoomer

There's someone new in the chat on zoomerfume.example. Zoomer is our AI helper, and it answers your
questions 24/7, even at night when our support team is offline.

## What you can ask

- **Perfume advice.** Tell Zoomer what you like, what you already wear or how much you want to spend,
  and it suggests perfumes from our catalog.
- **The catalog.** Notes, sizes, availability, samples and decants.
- **Shop rules.** Delivery, payment, returns, gift cards and Zoomer Points.
- **Notes for later.** Ask Zoomer to remember a perfume you liked, and it saves a note for you, like a
  wishlist.

When Zoomer gives you a rule, it shows which page of our site the answer comes from, so you can check
it yourself.

## What Zoomer can't do

Zoomer can't see orders, payments or accounts. It can't place or change an order, and it can't give
personal discounts. For anything about a specific order, it points you to our support team at
support@zoomerfume.example, every day from 9:00 to 21:00 Kyiv time.

## Why we built it

Most of the questions we get every day are the same: which perfume to pick, how delivery works, how
returns work. Zoomer answers those right away, at any hour. That gives our support team more time for
the orders that need a person.

Zoomer is an AI helper, so it can make mistakes. For anything important about an order, the support
team's word is the one that counts.

## Say hello

Open the chat on any page and ask Zoomer something. A good first question: "I love vanilla, but not
too sweet. What should I sample?" More about Zoomer in KB-064.
