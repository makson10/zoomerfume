## Overview

Baccarat Rouge 540 is one of the most recognizable niche perfumes of the last decade. Francis
Kurkdjian first created it in 2014 as a limited edition for the crystal house Baccarat, and his own
house released this Eau de Parfum in 2015. It's an airy, glowing blend of sugary amber and dry woods
with a slightly salty, mineral edge: sweet, but see-through rather than heavy. This card is for the Eau
de Parfum. The Extrait de Parfum has a different formula, and we don't carry it. We have only a few
bottles left. It's one of the niche perfumes we also sell as a 10 ml decant.

## Notes

Maison Francis Kurkdjian doesn't publish a classic pyramid. It names its key materials by accord:

- **Airy accord:** jasmine, saffron
- **Warm accord:** cedarwood
- **Mineral accord:** ambergris

Fragrance databases split it into a pyramid instead: saffron and jasmine on top, amberwood and
ambergris in the heart, fir resin and cedar in the base.

## How it wears

The first spray is airy and sweet, with a saffron glow and a hint of jasmine. It quickly turns into the
famous trail: candied amber and dry woods with a salty, almost mineral sparkle. After that it barely
changes, it just gets softer. On our team it lasted 10 hours or more, and it was often still on our
clothes the next day. The projection and trail are big. One thing to know: some people can't fully
smell its ambery woody materials and hardly notice it on themselves, even when others do.

## Who it's for

- Someone who wants a signature scent that people notice and remember.
- Evenings, events and special occasions, though it works all year round.
- Fans of sweet scents who want something airy and polished rather than sugary.

Who it's not for: anyone who wants a scent few people wear, because Baccarat Rouge 540 is everywhere.
For something quieter and more personal, see Escentric Molecules Molecule 01 (KB-027).
