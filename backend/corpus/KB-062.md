## One warehouse, in Kyiv

Every Zoomerfume order leaves from one place: our own warehouse in Kyiv. We don't ship from partner
warehouses or other shops. Whether you live in Lviv, Odesa or Kharkiv, your parcel starts its trip in
Kyiv, and so do our orders abroad (KB-045).

The warehouse isn't open to visitors. We have no shop, showroom or pickup point, so every order
reaches you by delivery (KB-059).

Eight people work there. They check new deliveries from our suppliers (KB-061), make the 10 ml decants
for niche perfumes, pack your orders and check returns when they arrive.

## How we pack an order

1. **Pick.** Your perfume is taken from the shelf and checked against the order: the right perfume,
   the right size and the right quantity.
2. **Check.** We look at the perfume's box once more. The factory cellophane must be intact. It shows
   the bottle is sealed, and it's also what makes a return possible later (KB-048).
3. **Extras.** Any samples you ordered go in. Every order from 1,500 ₴ also gets a free sample, which
   the team picks unless you asked for one in the order comment (KB-086). Gift orders go into our
   grape-and-pink gift box with a card and your message, and there's never a price inside a gift
   parcel (KB-053).
4. **Box.** Everything goes into a recycled cardboard box, with paper fill around the bottle so it
   can't move. The box is sealed with paper tape. We don't put plastic bubble wrap in our parcels
   (KB-066).
5. **Label and hand-over.** The parcel gets its delivery label and is handed to Nova Poshta, Ukrposhta
   or our own Kyiv courier.

As soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse, you get the tracking number by SMS and email (KB-072).

## Dispatch times

All times are Kyiv time.

- Orders paid by 15:00, Monday to Friday, leave the warehouse the same day. For cash on delivery, what
  counts is the time the order is confirmed.
- Orders paid after 15:00 leave the next working day.
- Orders placed on Saturday or Sunday leave on Monday.
- A public holiday moves dispatch to the next working day.

The one exception is our same-day courier in Kyiv. Orders placed by 13:00, Monday to Saturday, are
delivered the same evening, between 17:00 and 21:00 (KB-043).

## Need to change something?

An order can be changed or cancelled until it leaves the warehouse. Write to support with your order
number as early as you can (KB-073). Once the parcel is with the carrier, it can't be changed.
