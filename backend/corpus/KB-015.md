## Overview

Good Girl came out in 2016 in its famous stiletto-heel bottle. Carolina Herrera built it on a
contrast: heady white flowers over a dark, roasted base of tonka and cocoa, opened by almond and
coffee. It's rich, a little dramatic and clearly at its best in the evening. On our team it's the
scent people reach for when they want to feel dressed up, even in jeans. Lots of flankers and
collector bottles carry the Good Girl name, such as Very Good Girl and Good Girl Blush. This card is
for the original Eau de Parfum.

## Notes

- **Top:** almond, coffee
- **Heart:** jasmine sambac, tuberose
- **Base:** tonka bean, cocoa

The brand keeps its list short. Longer lists you may see elsewhere add notes like orange blossom,
rose, vanilla and sandalwood.

## How it wears

Almond and a touch of coffee come first, with a soft, marzipan-like sweetness that some people love
right away and others need a few minutes to get used to. Then the tuberose and jasmine bloom, creamy
and heady. After a couple of hours the tonka and cocoa take over, and it turns into a warm, slightly
powdery cloud. On our team it lasted 6–10 hours. It's strong in the first hours, then settles into a
warm aura that people notice when they hug you.

## Who it's for

- Evenings, dates, weddings and formal events.
- Fans of white flowers who want them darker and sweeter.
- Autumn and winter, when a rich scent feels right.

Who it's not for: the office, the gym or a hot day. For a cleaner jasmine and vanilla that works in
the daytime, try Valentino Born in Roma Donna (KB-017).

## Zoomer's take

The main-character energy is right there in the name and the heel. This is the perfume for when the
outfit is already loud. Funny thing: it doesn't smell like coffee for long. It's the tuberose and
cocoa that people remember, and ask about.
