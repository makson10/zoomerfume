## 27 people, one shop

Zoomerfume started in 2021 with two friends, Daryna and Oleh, and a shelf of their own bottles. Today
we're a team of 27 people, Daryna and Oleh included. Every bottle you order is chosen, checked and
packed by us, in Kyiv.

## Who does what

| Team | People | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Buyers | 3 | find authorized suppliers, order new deliveries and shape the catalog |
| Warehouse | 8 | check new deliveries, make decants, pack orders and check returns |
| Support | 6 | answer emails about orders, returns, refunds and complaints |
| Content and marketing | 6 | write the product cards and news, and run our sales |
| Tech | 4 | build and run the website and Zoomer |

- **Oleh**, co-founder, runs the warehouse. If your parcel left on time and arrived in one piece,

## How we choose what to sell

Before a perfume goes on sale, people across the team wear it for a few days: at work, at home and on
evenings out. What you read on a card about how long a scent lasted, or how far it carried, comes from
those days. Then we write down who the perfume is for and who should skip it.

That's how we keep the catalog at about 40 perfumes: the ones people actually wear now, from Gen-Z
favorites to niche icons and affordable Middle-Eastern brands. A perfume doesn't get in just because
it's famous.

## How we work with you

We want talking to Zoomerfume to feel like asking a friend who knows perfume. The support team writes
like people, not from a script. Content writes like a friend, not like a luxury boutique.
And Zoomer, our AI helper, answers the everyday questions at any hour, so the support team has more
time for the order problems that need a person.
