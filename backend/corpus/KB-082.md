## Short answer

Write to support@zoomerfume.example, and a person from our support team will answer. They're there
every day from 9:00 to 21:00 Kyiv time and usually reply within 2 hours. Messages sent outside those
hours get a reply the next morning.

## When to write to support

Zoomer, our AI helper, answers in the site chat 24/7, but it can't see orders, payments or accounts.
For anything about one specific order, write to support with your order number. That includes:

- where your order is, and parcels that are late
- changing or cancelling an order before it's dispatched
- returns and refunds
- damaged, defective or wrong items
- a question about a payment
- deleting your account and your data

## What to put in your email

- Your order number, if the question is about an order.
- Photos or a short video for a damaged, leaking or wrong item.
- The batch code from the box, if you'd like the distributor's certificate for a bottle.

Putting it all in the first email saves a round of questions, so you get your answer faster.

## When Zoomer is enough

Zoomer can answer questions about perfumes, our catalog and our shop rules, like delivery, payment,
returns and Zoomer Points. It can compare scents, suggest alternatives, and save a wishlist note when
you ask. It can't place, change or cancel orders, and it can't give personal discounts. When a
question needs a person, Zoomer will point you to support.

## Examples

- You write at 10:00 about a late parcel. Expect a reply by about noon.
- You write at 23:30 because the sprayer on your new bottle doesn't work. The team replies the next
  morning after 9:00. Defects need to be reported within 3 days of receiving the parcel, so it's
  worth writing as soon as you notice.
- You want to know whether a perfume suits winter. Zoomer can answer that right away, any time.

## Our team

Our support team is six people, part of the 27 who work at Zoomerfume. Every email is answered by
someone on that team.
