## Short answer

Start with a sample. A 2 ml vial costs 69 ₴, or 119 ₴ for niche perfumes, and it's enough to wear a
scent for a few days. To narrow the list first, read the "who it's for" part of each card or ask
Zoomer in the site chat.

## Narrow it down

- **Our product cards.** Every card describes how the scent wears in plain words, who it's for and
  who it's *not* for. The "not for" line is often the fastest way to cross a perfume off your list.
  Each card also suggests similar perfumes in our shop.
- **Zoomer.** Our AI helper answers in the site chat 24/7. Tell it what you already like, your
  budget and when you'd wear the scent, then ask for a shortlist or a comparison of two perfumes.
  Zoomer can also save the ones you like as a wishlist note.

## Try it before the full bottle

- **Samples:** 2 ml spray vials of any perfume we have in stock, up to 5 per order.
- **Decants:** 10 ml travel atomizers of our niche perfumes, for when a sample isn't enough to decide
  on an expensive bottle. The price is on each niche card.
- **The free sample:** every order from 1,500 ₴ comes with one. Name the perfume you'd like in the
  order comment, or let our team pick it (see KB-086).

Samples and decants can't be returned, but they cost far less than a bottle you're not sure about.
And if you buy the full bottle within 30 days of buying its sample, the sample price comes back to
you as Zoomer Points (see KB-052).

## An example

You like warm vanilla scents but don't know where to start.

1. Ask Zoomer for three options within your budget.
2. Open each card and read the "who it's not for" line. Drop anything that sounds like you.
3. Order samples of the ones that are left, all in one order.
4. Wear one per day, on your own skin, through a normal day.
5. Order the bottle you kept reaching for. If it's within 30 days of the sample order, you get the
   sample price back as points.

## Already have a favorite?

Ask Zoomer for something similar, or check the "Similar in our shop" list on its card. If your
favorite is a perfume we don't carry, Zoomer can still suggest the closest ones from our catalog.
