## Overview

Lost Cherry is part of Tom Ford's Private Blend collection. It was created by Louise Turner and
launched in 2018. It's a sweet, boozy cherry scent with bitter almond and warm tonka, often
described as a cherry liqueur dessert in a bottle. It's marketed as unisex and wears well on anyone
who likes sweetness. We also carry its sibling from the same collection, Tobacco Vanille (KB-035),
which is warm and sweet too but has no fruit at all. Lost Cherry is one of the niche perfumes we
also sell as a 10 ml decant.

## Notes

Tom Ford lists key notes rather than a full pyramid:

- **Key notes:** black cherry accord, bitter almond, griotte (sour cherry) syrup, rose absolute, Peru
  balsam, roasted tonka

Fan databases split it into top, heart and base, and they also mention cherry liqueur, plum, jasmine
and vanilla.

## How it wears

It opens with dark, juicy cherry and a marzipan-like bitter almond, like cherry liqueur poured over
a dessert. The rose is soft and stays in the background. As it dries down, the fruit fades and the
scent turns into a smooth, balsamic tonka base.

Wear time is where opinions split. On our team, some people got 6–8 hours. For others, the bright
cherry was gone after about an hour, and only a soft almond and tonka skin scent stayed. Projection
is moderate, so it rarely fills a room.

## Who it's for

- Fans of sweet, gourmand scents who want something more grown-up than candy.
- Evenings, dates and parties in autumn and winter.
- A statement gift for someone who already loves cherry or almond.

Who it's not for: people who find marzipan or syrupy sweetness too much, and anyone who needs a
scent that's sure to last all day. For a warm, sweet scent without the fruit and with stronger
wear, try Tobacco Vanille (KB-035).
