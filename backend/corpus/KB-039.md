## Overview

Club de Nuit Intense Man is Armaf's best-known perfume, launched in 2015 in a black bottle. It's
often compared with Creed Aventus (KB-028), because both pair a fruity pineapple and citrus opening
with smoky birch. It's a perfume in its own right, with its own character and a much friendlier
price. This card is for the original Eau de Toilette. Armaf also makes smoother EDP and Parfum
versions, which we don't carry. Armaf joined our catalog on 12 March 2025 (see KB-087).

## Notes

- **Top:** apple, bergamot, blackcurrant, pineapple, lemon
- **Heart:** rose, birch, jasmine
- **Base:** musk, ambergris, patchouli, vanilla

## How it wears

The first hour is loud: sharp lemon and tart pineapple over a strong, smoky birch. That's the part
people either love right away or need a day or two to get used to. After that it calms down into
smoky woods and a soft, musky, ambery base. On our team it lasted 8–10 hours, with a strong trail for
the first few.

Next to Aventus, the shared parts are the pineapple and citrus opening, the smoky birch and the clean
musky dry-down. The differences: Club de Nuit is sharper and smokier at the start, while Aventus is
smoother, fruitier and more polished. Aventus also costs many times more.

Like Aventus, it's known for small differences between production batches, so two bottles can open
slightly differently. That's normal for this perfume. Every bottle we sell comes from an authorized
source (see KB-051).

## Who it's for

- Someone who loves the Aventus style but not the Aventus price.
- One signature scent for evenings, weekends and, with a light hand, the office.
- Spring and autumn, plus mild winter days.

Who it's not for: anyone who wants a soft or sweet scent, or who dislikes smoky notes. For a sweet
evening scent at a similar price, try Afnan 9PM (KB-040).
