## Overview

Libre Intense came out in 2020, a year after the original Libre, and it was made by the same
perfumers, Anne Flipo and Carlos Benaïm. It's a separate perfume with its own formula, not just a
stronger copy of Libre Eau de Parfum (KB-012). It keeps the lavender and orange blossom idea but adds
an orchid accord, tonka bean and more vanilla, so it feels warmer, rounder and creamier.

How it differs from Libre Eau de Parfum:

- Warmer and sweeter, with tonka and vanilla in the lead.
- Less fresh at the start. The lavender is still there, but it's rounder and softer.
- Made for autumn and winter. The original also works in spring.
- Costs a little more: the 30 ml is 3,190 ₴ here and 2,590 ₴ for the original.

## Notes

- **Top:** mandarin, French lavender, bergamot
- **Heart:** lavender, jasmine sambac, Moroccan orange blossom, orchid accord
- **Base:** Madagascar vanilla, Venezuelan tonka bean, ambergris, vetiver

Lavender appears twice in the brand's list: a bright one on top and a deeper one in the heart.

## How it wears

The opening is a softer version of Libre: mandarin and lavender, but already a little creamy. Orange
blossom and orchid come through quickly, and the tonka and vanilla arrive much sooner than in the
original. On our team it lasted 7–10 hours, about the same as Libre Eau de Parfum. The difference is in
the feel, not the hours: denser and warmer, more like a soft sweater than a crisp shirt.

## Who it's for

- Someone who loves Libre but wants it cozier for cold weather.
- Evenings, dinners and winter days when a fresh scent feels too thin.
- Fans of creamy, ambery florals who still don't want pure dessert.

Who it's not for: anyone looking for a fresh scent for spring or a warm office. The original Libre Eau
de Parfum (KB-012) is the brighter pick.

## Zoomer's take

Libre Intense is Libre in its winter coat. Same personality, more warmth. If the original felt a bit
sharp on you, this might be your one. Honest tip: get samples of both, because some people barely
notice the difference and others swear they're two totally different perfumes.
