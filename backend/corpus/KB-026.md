## Overview

Jazz Club is the Replica scent of a late night in a dim jazz lounge: rum in a glass, leather armchairs
and sweet pipe tobacco in the air. It launched in 2013 and was created by Aliénor Massenet. It was the
first men's scent in Maison Margiela's Replica line, and today it's widely worn as unisex. The heart of
it is a trio of rum, tobacco leaf and vanilla. At 2,490 ₴ for 30 ml, it's the most affordable
tobacco-and-vanilla scent in our shop. Its Replica sibling, By the Fireplace (KB-025), is smoky and
woody instead.

## Notes

- **Top:** pink pepper, Primofiore lemon, neroli
- **Heart:** rum absolute, clary sage, Java vetiver
- **Base:** tobacco leaf absolute, vanilla bean, styrax

Some fragrance databases also list tonka bean in the base.

## How it wears

It starts with a quick, peppery citrus sparkle. Then the rum comes through: smooth, boozy and a little
sweet. After about an hour the tobacco leaf and vanilla take over, with a dry, slightly smoky edge from
the vetiver and styrax. It stays warm and sweet, never ashy or harsh. On our team it lasted 5–8 hours.
It projects less than its reputation suggests: a nice trail for the first couple of hours, then a soft,
warm cloud close to the body.

## Who it's for

- Fans of tobacco and vanilla who want a smooth, easy version at a friendly price.
- Cool evenings, casual dates and cozy weekends, for any gender.
- Anyone who likes sweet scents with a grown-up, smoky twist.

Who it's not for: people looking for a fresh summer scent, or for a huge trail. For a much more
powerful tobacco and vanilla, see Tom Ford Tobacco Vanille (KB-035).
