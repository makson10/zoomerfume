## Overview

Sauvage Elixir is the most concentrated version of Dior's Sauvage line, launched in 2021 and
composed by François Demachy. Despite the name, it has little in common with the bright Eau de
Toilette (KB-001). It's dark, spicy and dense, built around cinnamon, lavender and licorice. It's
the third Sauvage we carry, next to the EDT and the Eau de Parfum (KB-002). Dior labels its strength
simply as "Elixir". It's a different perfume from Sauvage Parfum, which we don't carry.

## Notes

- **Top:** grapefruit, cinnamon, nutmeg, cardamom
- **Heart:** lavender
- **Base:** licorice, woody notes, patchouli, vetiver

Some lists name the woods in the base as sandalwood and amber.

## How it wears

The spices hit first: cinnamon and cardamom, with a squeeze of grapefruit that fades within minutes.
The lavender in the middle is rich and almost herbal-sweet, and the base turns to licorice over warm
woods. Next to the other two Sauvage versions, it's the least fresh and the most spicy, and it's by
far the strongest of the three. On our team it lasted 10–12 hours on skin and even longer on a scarf
or a jacket. One or two sprays are plenty. More than that can fill a small room.

## Who it's for

- Sauvage fans who want something darker and more grown-up for the evening.
- Autumn and winter: dates, dinners and nights out.
- Someone who wants a bottle to last, since you need very little per wear.

Who it's not for: hot days, small offices, or anyone who loves Sauvage for its clean freshness. For
that, take the Eau de Toilette (KB-001) instead.

## Zoomer's take

Sauvage after dark. Spicy, rich and a little dramatic, and a 60 ml bottle goes a long way because
two sprays carry you through the whole night. We're down to the last few bottles, so if you've been
eyeing it, try a sample now and don't sleep on it for too long. Cold-weather only, though. In July
it's way too much.
