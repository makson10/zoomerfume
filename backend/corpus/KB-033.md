## Overview

Santal 33 is Le Labo's best-known perfume and one of the most recognizable niche scents of the
last fifteen years. It was created by Frank Voelkl and launched in 2011. The brand writes the name
in capitals, SANTAL 33. It's built
around dry sandalwood and cedar with a leathery, slightly spicy edge, and it's worn by men and women
alike. It's one of the niche perfumes we also sell as a 10 ml decant.

## Notes

Le Labo doesn't publish a top, heart and base. It names five key notes:

- **Key notes:** cardamom, iris, violet, Australian sandalwood, cedarwood

The leathery side you'll notice on skin isn't on the official list. Fan databases add leather,
papyrus and amber.

## How it wears

The first minutes are spicy cardamom with a soft, powdery touch of iris and violet. Soon the woods
take over: creamy sandalwood, dry cedar that reminds many people of pencil shavings, and a note of
worn leather. A few people notice a slightly sour, almost pickle-like facet at the start, and it
usually fades quickly. On our team it lasted 7–10 hours. It projects moderately at first, then
settles into a close, skin-like aura that still leaves a trail people recognize.

## Who it's for

- Fans of dry, woody scents who want something polished rather than sweet.
- Couples or friends who like to share one bottle, since it suits everyone equally well.
- Everyday city wear, the office and weekends, best in spring and autumn.

Who it's not for: anyone who wants a sweet or cozy scent, or who'd rather not smell like a perfume
they keep meeting in cafés. For something warmer and smokier, try Replica By the Fireplace
(KB-025).

## Zoomer's take

Smells like a leather sofa in a very aesthetic coffee shop, in the best way. It's everywhere for a
reason, but it can turn a little sour on some skin, so test it before you commit. The 10 ml decant
is the low-risk move. If you end up loving it, the 100 ml bottle is a much better deal per ml than
the 50 ml.
