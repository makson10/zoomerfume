## What's changed

From 1 October 2026, our prices for Creed and Parfums de Marly are higher. Our distributors have sent
us new price lists for both houses, and our prices follow them.

The product cards already show the new prices:

- Creed Aventus (KB-028)
- Creed Silver Mountain Water (KB-029)
- Parfums de Marly Layton (KB-030)
- Parfums de Marly Delina (KB-031)

Each card lists the current price of every size and of the 10 ml decant, so that's the place to check
before you order.

## What stays the same

- A 2 ml sample of any of these four still costs 119 ₴, like every niche sample (KB-052).
- This update doesn't change the prices of any other brand in our catalog.
- If you paid for an order before 1 October, including a pre-order of Silver Mountain Water, you keep
  the price you paid.

## Thinking about Aventus or Layton?

If the new price makes you pause, start with a 10 ml decant. It's enough to wear the perfume for a
few weeks before you decide on a full bottle.

Looking for the Aventus style at a lower price? Armaf Club de Nuit Intense Man (KB-039) is the
best-known alternative in our catalog. It's smokier and sharper at the start, but it's built around
the same pineapple and birch idea.

Questions about any of these perfumes? Ask Zoomer in the site chat (KB-064), or see the full price
list on each card.
