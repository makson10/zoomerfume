## Overview

Bleu de Chanel Eau de Parfum came out in 2014, composed by Jacques Polge. It's the warmer, smoother
version of the original 2010 Bleu de Chanel: clean citrus over creamy sandalwood and cedar, with a
soft amber warmth. It's one of the most versatile men's scents we sell, and it's often compared head
to head with Dior Sauvage (KB-001 and KB-002). We carry only the Eau de Parfum. The Eau de Toilette,
the Parfum and the newer L'Exclusif are different versions and aren't in our catalog.

## Notes

Chanel doesn't publish a strict pyramid. This is how the brand describes the scent:

- **Top:** citrus notes
- **Heart:** cedar with an amber touch
- **Base:** New Caledonian sandalwood, tonka bean, vanilla

Longer lists you may see elsewhere add grapefruit, ginger, incense and labdanum, but Chanel doesn't
name those.

## How it wears

It opens with bright, clean citrus, then quickly turns smooth and woody. The sandalwood gives it a
creamy feel, and a soft, slightly smoky amber warmth stays in the background. It's polished and
never loud. On our team it lasted 7–9 hours, with moderate projection: people notice it when they're
close to you, not from across the room. It works as well on a hot summer day as on a winter evening.

## Who it's for

- Someone who wants one safe, elegant bottle for work, formal events and weekends.
- Any season. It's one of the most office-friendly scents in our men's range.
- Fans of fresh scents who want something smoother and woodier than Sauvage.

Who it's not for: anyone who wants a sweet, attention-grabbing scent for clubs and parties. For
that, try Versace Eros Eau de Toilette (KB-010).

## Zoomer's take

The "dresses well without trying" scent. It's not out to shock anyone, it just smells expensive and
put-together, from a job interview to a wedding. If you only want one bottle, this is a strong
candidate. Start with the 50 ml before you go all in on the 150 ml.
