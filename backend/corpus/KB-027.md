## Overview

Molecule 01 is one of the most unusual perfumes we sell: the whole formula is a single aroma molecule,
Iso E Super. It was created by Geza Schoen and launched in 2006. Instead of a story told in top, heart
and base notes, it gives a soft, velvety, faintly woody haze that blends with the wearer's own body
chemistry, so it smells a little unique on each person. Many people say it smells like their natural
scent, only warmer and cleaner. Don't confuse it with Escentric 01, the brand's blended version, which
we don't carry. Molecule 01 is one of the niche perfumes we also sell as a 10 ml decant.

## Notes

Molecule 01 has no pyramid. It's built from one aroma material:

- **Top, heart and base:** Iso E Super, a woody, velvety molecule

Escentric Molecules files it under woody and musk. Apart from the molecule, the bottle holds only
alcohol and water.

## How it wears

There's no real opening or dry-down: it smells much the same from the first minute to the last. Up
close it's a clean, faintly woody warmth with a soft musky glow. Its quirk is that it comes and goes.
Many wearers stop noticing it on themselves after a while, while people around them still catch it
when they lean in. On our team it lasted all day on the body and even longer on a scarf or a sweater.
The trail is low to moderate and stays close.

## Who it's for

- People who want to smell good in a quiet, personal way, not "perfumed".
- Minimalists, and anyone who wants one easy scent for every season, from the office to the weekend.
- Perfume fans who like to wear it under another fragrance, as a soft woody base.

Who it's not for: anyone who wants a scent with a clear story and a big trail, or who needs to smell
their own perfume all day. For a bold, instantly recognizable signature, see Maison Francis Kurkdjian
Baccarat Rouge 540 Eau de Parfum (KB-032).
