## Short answer

Yes. Zoomer Points expire 24 months after they arrive in your account. Every batch of points has its
own expiry date, and you can see all of them on your account page.

## How the 24 months are counted

Points arrive 14 days after your order is delivered, once the return window has passed. The 24
months start on the day the points arrive, not on the day you placed the order.

For example, an order delivered on 3 March 2025 brings its points on 17 March 2025. Those points can
be used until 17 March 2027.

## Each batch has its own date

Points from different orders don't share one expiry date. Say you order in March and again in May.
The March points expire two years after they arrived in March. The May points arrive later, so they
also expire later. A new order doesn't extend the date of the points you already have.

The 24 months run whether you shop often or not, and the rule is the same for every member.

## Where to see your points

Sign in on zoomerfume.example with your phone number and open your account page. It lists each
batch of points with its own expiry date, so you can see which points will run out first.

## How not to lose them

- Check your account page now and then, especially before a bigger order.
- Points can be spent once you have at least 100, and they can pay for up to 30% of the products
  total (see KB-055).
- 1 point is worth 1 ₴ on your next order.
- Points can't be exchanged for cash, so the only way to get value from them is to use them before
  they expire.

## What happens when points expire

Expired points are removed from your balance on their expiry date. Points that haven't reached their
date stay in your account as usual.

## Something looks wrong?

If a batch of points is missing, or a date doesn't look right, write to support@zoomerfume.example
with your order number. Our support team works every day from 9:00 to 21:00 Kyiv time and usually
replies within 2 hours.
