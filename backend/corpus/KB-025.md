## Overview

By the Fireplace is part of Maison Margiela's Replica line, a collection of scents built around
specific memories. This one is a winter evening at home in front of an open fire: wood smoke, roasted
chestnuts and warm vanilla. It launched in 2015 and was created by Marie Salamagne. It's a unisex Eau
de Toilette. We carry two Replica scents, and people often mix them up. This one is smoky and woody,
while Jazz Club (KB-026) is boozy and sweet, built around rum, tobacco and vanilla.

## Notes

- **Top:** pink pepper, orange flower, clove oil
- **Heart:** chestnut accord, guaiac wood oil, cade oil
- **Base:** vanilla accord, Peru balsam, cashmeran

Cade oil is the smoky wood oil behind the fire effect. Some fragrance databases list juniper in its
place.

## How it wears

The opening is spicy and a little sharp, with clove and pink pepper. Then the smoke rolls in: dry,
woody and slightly tarry, with sweet roasted chestnut underneath. For some people the smoke is strong
in the first half hour. After that it softens into vanilla and balsam, with just a glow of embers left.
On our team it lasted 5–7 hours. The trail is moderate for the first few hours, and then it becomes a
warm, cozy scent close to the body.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who loves the smell of a wood fire, frosty evenings and cozy weekends.
- Fans of warm, sweet scents who want something smoky rather than sugary.
- Autumn and winter: the holidays, walks in frosty weather, long evenings at home.

Who it's not for: people who don't like smoky scents, or who want something fresh for warm days. For a
cozy scent with no smoke at all, try Ariana Grande Cloud Eau de Parfum (KB-022).
