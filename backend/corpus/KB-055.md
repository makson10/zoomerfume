## Summary

Zoomer Points are our loyalty points, and 1 point = 1 ₴. You earn 5%, 7% or 10% of what you pay for
products, depending on your tier, and the points arrive 14 days after delivery. You can use them from
100 points, for up to 30% of the products total. Points that arrive on or after 1 September 2026
expire 12 months after they arrive. Points that arrived before that date keep the old 24-month term.

## Who can collect points

Points need a Zoomerfume account, which you sign in to with your phone number. You can also order as
a guest, but guest orders don't earn points (see KB-083).

## Earning points

- **Rate:** a percentage of the amount you pay for products, after any discounts. Fresh members earn
  5%, Hype 7% and Icon 10% (see KB-056). Points are rounded down to a whole point.
- **What doesn't earn points:** delivery, buying a gift card, and the part of an order you pay with
  points. The part you pay with a gift card earns points as usual, because a gift card works like money.
- **When they arrive:** 14 days after your parcel is delivered, once the return window has passed.
- **On product cards:** the points shown are for the Fresh tier (5%). Hype and Icon members get more.

### Birthday points

Add your birthday to your account, and every year in your birthday month you get a gift of 200
points.

## Using points

- **Minimum:** you can use points once you have at least 100. The smallest amount you can use in one
  order is 100 points.
- **Limit:** points can cover up to 30% of the products total. You pay the rest with any payment
  method you like.
- **Value:** 1 point = 1 ₴.
- **With other savings:** points work together with a promo code and with gift cards in the same
  order (see KB-057 and KB-053).
- **Free delivery:** the free-delivery thresholds look at the products total after points (see
  KB-042).

The step-by-step checkout guide is in KB-076.

## Expiry

- Points that arrive on or after 1 September 2026 expire 12 months after they arrive.
- Points that arrived before 1 September 2026 keep the old rule and expire 24 months after they
  arrived.
- The rule is the same for all points, including birthday points.
- Your account page lists each batch of points with its own expiry date.

We announced this change on 3 August 2026 (see KB-093).

## Returns

- Points earned on a returned product are cancelled. Points only arrive 14 days after delivery, so
  in most cases they simply never appear.
- Points you used to pay for a returned product come back to your account (see KB-048).

## Points and cash

Points can only be used on orders in our shop. They can't be exchanged for cash or paid out.

## Examples

- You're in the Fresh tier and order Versace Eros Eau de Toilette 50 ml (KB-010) for 2,490 ₴. You
  pay 200 points and the remaining 2,290 ₴ by card. You earn 114 points, which is 5% of the 2,290 ₴
  you paid by card. Delivery to a Nova Poshta branch is still free, because 2,290 ₴ is over
  2,000 ₴.
- Your products total is 6,590 ₴, for Dior Sauvage Eau de Parfum 100 ml (KB-002). Points can cover up
  to 30% of it, which is 1,977 ₴. If you have more points than that, the rest stay in your account
  for later.
- You have 94 points. That's not enough to use yet: you need at least 100.
- Your order is delivered on 20 September 2026. Its points arrive on 4 October 2026 and expire on
  4 October 2027.
- Points that arrived on 10 August 2026 keep the old rule and expire on 10 August 2028.

## Edge cases

- **Points can take you under the free-delivery threshold.** You order a Le Labo Santal 33 decant
  for 2,190 ₴ and use 200 points. The products total is now 1,990 ₴, so Nova Poshta branch delivery
  costs 79 ₴.
- **Checking your balance.** Your balance and each batch's expiry date are on your account page.
  Zoomer can't see your account, so write to support@zoomerfume.example with any question about
  your own points.
