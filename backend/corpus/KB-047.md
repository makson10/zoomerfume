## Summary

Orders of 3,000 ₴ or more can be paid in 3 interest-free monthly payments through our partner banks.
You choose pay in parts at checkout, and the bank approves it instantly. You need a card from one of
our partner banks. You pay the same price as with a regular card payment.

## Who can use it

- **Order total:** 3,000 ₴ or more. Smaller orders can't be split.
- **Card:** a card issued by one of our partner banks. Checkout shows which banks take part. Cards
  from other banks can't be used for pay in parts, but you can still pay with them in full.
- **Approval:** the partner bank decides, instantly, while you're still at checkout. We don't see or
  influence the bank's decision.

## How it works

1. Add your products to the cart. Once the total reaches 3,000 ₴, the pay-in-parts option appears at
   checkout.
2. Choose pay in parts and pick your partner bank.
3. Confirm the payment plan with the bank. It shows you the schedule before you agree.
4. The bank approves the plan instantly, and your order is confirmed by email and SMS.
5. You make 3 monthly payments to the bank. There's no interest, and we add nothing to the price.

## What it means for your order

- **It counts as prepaid.** For us, the order is paid as soon as the bank approves it. It leaves the
  warehouse on the usual schedule: paid by 15:00 on a weekday, it leaves the same day (KB-041).
- **Any delivery option.** Because the order is prepaid, you can choose any delivery, including
  Ukrposhta and international shipping, which are prepaid only (KB-044, KB-045).
- **Points and free delivery work as usual.** Zoomer Points are earned on the amount paid for
  products, after discounts (KB-055). The free-delivery amounts are the same as for any other order
  (KB-042).

## Questions about your payments

The payment plan is an agreement between you and the partner bank. For questions about the payment
dates, the amount left to pay or a missed payment, contact your bank. For questions about the order
itself, write to support@zoomerfume.example (KB-063).

## Cancellations and returns

- **Before dispatch:** you can cancel the order by writing to support with your order number. The
  partner bank cancels the plan and returns what you've already paid.
- **After delivery:** returns follow the usual rules: unopened, with the cellophane intact, within 14
  days of receipt (KB-048). When we confirm the return, the partner bank cancels the remaining
  payments and returns what you've already paid.

## Examples

- An order of 3,000 ₴: three monthly payments of 1,000 ₴, with no interest.
- Creed Aventus 50 ml (12,490 ₴): you pay it in three interest-free monthly payments. Since the order
  is over 10,000 ₴, it can't be paid on delivery anyway, so pay in parts is a handy option.
- Rabanne Lady Million 50 ml (2,990 ₴): just under 3,000 ₴, so pay in parts isn't offered. Adding a
  2 ml sample (69 ₴) brings the total over 3,000 ₴.
- You returned the bottle after paying the first part. The bank cancels the two remaining payments
  and gives back the one you made.

## Edge cases

- Pay in parts is an online payment, so it can't be combined with cash on delivery.
- If the bank doesn't approve the plan, nothing is charged. Choose another payment method, such as a
  card, Apple Pay or Google Pay (KB-046).
- Buying a gift for someone else? A pay-in-parts order is prepaid, so the recipient pays nothing on
  delivery (KB-080).
- Zoomer, our AI helper, can explain how pay in parts works, but it can't see your payment plan or
  the bank's decision (KB-064).
