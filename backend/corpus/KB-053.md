## Summary

Gift wrapping is free on any order. We pack the perfume in our grape-and-pink gift box with a card
carrying your message, and there's no price anywhere inside the parcel. Gift cards are electronic and
come in 500, 1,000, 2,000, 3,000 and 5,000 ₴. We email them to you or straight to the recipient on
the date you choose, and they're valid for 12 months.

## Gift wrapping

- **Free,** on any order.
- **What's inside:** the perfume in its original, sealed box goes into our grape-and-pink gift box,
  together with a card with your message. The parcel itself is our usual recycled cardboard box.
- **No price:** we never put a price inside a gift parcel, so the person opening it won't see what
  it cost. The order confirmation goes only to you, by email and SMS.
- **How to add it:** choose gift wrapping at checkout and type the message for the card.

## Sending a gift to another address

1. Add the perfume to your cart and choose gift wrapping.
2. Enter the recipient's name and their delivery details: a Nova Poshta branch or parcel locker, an
   address for the courier, or a Ukrposhta office.
3. Pay online. With prepayment, the recipient pays nothing when they collect the parcel. With cash on
   delivery, they would have to pay for the order when they collect it, so it isn't a good choice
   for a gift.
4. Tell them a parcel is on its way. A Nova Poshta parcel waits 5 days free of charge, then the
   carrier starts charging for storage (see KB-075).

## Gift cards

- **Amounts:** 500, 1,000, 2,000, 3,000 or 5,000 ₴.
- **Electronic only:** a gift card is a code sent by email. There's nothing to post.
- **Delivery date:** we send it to you or directly to the recipient, on the date you pick at
  checkout. That can be today or a date in the future.
- **Valid for 12 months** from the day it's sent.
- **Spend it in parts:** a gift card can pay for several orders. Whatever isn't used stays on the
  card until it expires.
- **Not for cash:** a gift card can't be exchanged for money, and it can't be returned (see KB-048).
- **No points on buying one:** buying a gift card doesn't earn Zoomer Points (see KB-055).

### Paying with a gift card

1. At checkout, enter the gift card code.
2. The card's balance pays for the order. If the order costs more, pay the rest the usual way.
3. You can use a gift card together with Zoomer Points and a promo code in the same order (see
   KB-084 and KB-057).

A gift card works like money, so the free-delivery threshold looks at the products total after the
gift card is taken off. See the examples below.

## Examples

- You send Lancôme La Vie Est Belle 50 ml (3,390 ₴) to your sister in Lviv. You choose gift
  wrapping, write a message for the card and pay online. Delivery to her Nova Poshta branch is free,
  because the order is over 2,000 ₴. She pays nothing, and there's no price in the parcel.
- On 20 February you buy a 2,000 ₴ gift card for a friend and set the delivery date to 8 March. She
  gets the email on 8 March, and the card is valid for 12 months from that day.
- You order Versace Eros Eau de Toilette 50 ml for 2,490 ₴ and pay 500 ₴ of it with a gift card. The
  products total is now 1,990 ₴, under the 2,000 ₴ threshold, so Nova Poshta branch delivery costs
  79 ₴.
- Your friend uses her 5,000 ₴ gift card on a 30 ml bottle in March and on three samples in June.
  Both orders come off the same card.

## Edge cases

- **The gift card email didn't arrive.** Check the spam folder first. If it isn't there, write to
  support@zoomerfume.example with your order number, and we'll send it again.
- **Returning a gift-wrapped perfume.** Our gift box may be opened, but the perfume's own cellophane
  must be intact (see KB-048).
