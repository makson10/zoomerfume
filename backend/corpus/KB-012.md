## Overview

Libre is the women's fragrance Yves Saint Laurent launched in 2019, created by Anne Flipo and Carlos
Benaïm. Its idea is a twist: it takes the lavender you usually find in men's fougères and pairs it
with orange blossom and vanilla. The result smells polished and confident rather than sugary. We carry
two versions of it: this Eau de Parfum and the warmer, creamier Libre Intense (KB-013). Of the two,
this one is fresher and brighter, and it's the easier pick for daytime and spring.

## Notes

- **Top:** mandarin, neroli, French lavender
- **Heart:** jasmine sambac, Moroccan orange blossom
- **Base:** white musks, Madagascar vanilla

This is the brand's own list. Some shops put the lavender in the heart instead, and a few longer lists
add blackcurrant, cedar and ambergris.

## How it wears

It opens with juicy mandarin and a clear, slightly herbal lavender. Some people find that lavender a
bit sharp for the first few minutes, while others love that it isn't another sweet opening. Within an
hour the orange blossom takes over, and the dry-down is smooth vanilla and clean musk. On our team it
lasted 6–10 hours, with a noticeable trail for the first few hours and a soft, close finish after that.

## Who it's for

- Someone who wants a grown-up signature scent that works at the office and still feels right in the
  evening.
- Fans of orange blossom who find most floral perfumes too sweet or too soft.
- Autumn, winter and spring. On a hot summer day it can feel a little thick.

Who it's not for: anyone who really dislikes lavender. For jasmine and vanilla with no lavender at all,
try Valentino Born in Roma Donna (KB-017).
