## Overview

Burberry launched Goddess in 2023. It's a vanilla perfume built in layers: the brand uses three
different vanilla materials and adds lavender to stop it from turning into dessert. Burberry calls it
a gourmand aromatic. It's warm, soft and comforting, but it still smells put together. It's one of the
newer perfumes in our women's range, and it quickly became a team favorite for cold-weather days. The
line has grown since then, with Goddess Intense, Goddess Parfum and more. This card is for the
original Eau de Parfum.

## Notes

- **Top:** vanilla infusion, lavender
- **Heart:** vanilla caviar
- **Base:** vanilla absolute

Burberry also names cocoa and ginger root as supporting notes. Some lists put them in the top.

## How it wears

Lavender and soft vanilla are there right from the start. The lavender stays clear for the first hour
or so, then the vanilla deepens and turns creamier. Most people barely notice the cocoa and ginger.
It's fairly linear, so what you smell at noon is close to what you smell in the evening. On our team
it lasted 8–10 hours, with a moderate trail that gets noticed without filling the room. That makes it
soft enough for the office, too.

## Who it's for

- Vanilla lovers who want it grown-up instead of sugary.
- Cozy days and cold evenings in autumn and winter.
- Someone who likes lavender but finds Libre (KB-012) too floral. Goddess keeps the lavender and
  vanilla and drops most of the flowers.

Who it's not for: anyone who doesn't like vanilla, or who wants something fresh for summer. For a
sheer floral, see Marc Jacobs Daisy (KB-020).

## Zoomer's take

Goddess is vanilla on vanilla on vanilla, and somehow it totally works. The lavender is doing a lot of
quiet work, so you smell polished, not like a bakery. It's also one of the friendliest prices for this
vibe: the 30 ml is 2,290 ₴.
