## Overview

Silver Mountain Water is Creed's crisp, icy citrus scent, launched in 1995. Sparkling bergamot and tart
blackcurrant rest on green tea and soft musk, and the effect is like clear water running over stones
high in the mountains, with snow still on the peaks. It's unisex. Some bottles and shops still use
Creed's older "Millésime" label, and that's the same perfume. It's on pre-order right now. It's one of
the niche perfumes we also sell as a 10 ml decant. Our Creed prices were updated on 1 October 2026
(see KB-095).

## Notes

Creed rewrote its official note list in recent years. This is the current one:

- **Top:** bergamot, blackcurrant, galbanum, orange
- **Heart:** tea, ozonic accord
- **Base:** musk, sandalwood

Older lists that you may still see elsewhere mention mandarin, neroli and green tea instead.

## How it wears

The opening is bright and fizzy: bergamot and orange with the tart, slightly sour snap of blackcurrant
and a green edge from galbanum. Then the tea and the airy ozonic note come forward, and the scent turns
cool and almost metallic, like frost or chilled spring water. The base is clean, soft musk with a
little sandalwood. On our team it lasted 5–8 hours. It projects nicely for the first hour, then stays
fairly close.

## Who it's for

- People who want a fresh scent that feels refined rather than sporty.
- Hot days, spring and summer, the office and clean, casual daytime wear.
- Anyone who loves citrus and green tea but wants more depth than a typical fresh perfume.

Who it's not for: anyone who wants something warm, sweet or long-trailing. For a bolder Creed with more
presence, see Aventus (KB-028).
