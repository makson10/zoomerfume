## How to reach us

- **Email:** support@zoomerfume.example
- **Support hours:** every day, 9:00–21:00 Kyiv time, weekends included
- **Reply time:** within 2 hours during support hours. If you write after 21:00 or at night, we reply
  the next morning.
- **Site chat:** Zoomer, our AI helper, answers 24/7 (KB-064)

We're an online-only shop, so there's no shop or office to visit (KB-059). Our support team answers
by email.

## What the support team handles

Write to support about anything to do with a specific order. The team can see your order and act on
it:

- **Order status.** Where your order is and when it left our warehouse.
- **Changes and cancellations.** Possible until the order is dispatched (KB-073).
- **Returns.** We send you a return number and our return address by email (KB-048).
- **Refunds.** Questions about a refund for a return or an unclaimed parcel.
- **Complaints.** A wrong item or a defect you found at home: write within 3 days of receipt, with
  photos or a video. A parcel damaged in transit is best checked at the branch first (KB-050).
- **A parcel that's taking too long** (KB-074).
- **Authenticity questions.** Send us the batch code, and we'll send you the distributor's certificate
  (KB-051).
- **Zoomer Points on your account.** For example, points from an order that didn't arrive.
- **Your personal data.** For example, deleting your account (KB-058).

## What to put in your email

- Your order number. You'll find it in the confirmation email and SMS.
- What happened and what you'd like us to do.
- Photos or a video, if something is damaged, wrong or not working.
- The batch code from the box, if you're asking whether a bottle is original.

With these details, we can usually solve things in one reply.

## What Zoomer handles

Zoomer, the AI helper in our site chat, is there 24/7 for everything that isn't about one specific
order:

- choosing a perfume, comparing scents and finding something within your budget
- what's in the catalog: notes, sizes, prices and availability
- shop rules: delivery, payment, returns, samples, gift cards and Zoomer Points
- saving notes for you, like a wishlist

Zoomer can't see orders, payments or accounts. It can't place or change an order, and it can't give
personal discounts. For those, it points you to the support team.

Not sure who to ask? Start with Zoomer in the chat. If your question needs someone who can see your
order, Zoomer will tell you to email support.
