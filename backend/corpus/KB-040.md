## Overview

9PM is a sweet men's evening scent from Afnan, part of the brand's 9am and 9pm line. Some shops sell
it as "9PM Black", and that's the same perfume. It opens with apple, cinnamon and lavender and dries
down to creamy vanilla and amber. As the name suggests, it's made for nights out rather than
mornings at the office. Afnan has also released several flankers, such as 9 PM Rebel and 9PM
Elixir. This card is for the original Eau de Parfum. Afnan joined our catalog on 12 March 2025, together with Lattafa and Armaf
(see KB-087).

## Notes

- **Top:** bergamot, lavandin, cinnamon, apple
- **Heart:** lily of the valley, orange blossom
- **Base:** patchouli, amber, vanilla, tonka bean

## How it wears

It opens loud and sweet: crisp apple and warm cinnamon over a herbal lavender. For a moment it's
almost like a warm apple dessert, but the lavender keeps it from reading as purely sweet. The floral
heart is light, and it mostly keeps the sweetness from getting sticky. The dry-down is creamy vanilla, amber
and tonka with a little earthy patchouli.

On our team it lasted 8–10 hours, with strong projection for the first few hours. It's at its best
in cold air and crowded rooms. On a warm day or in a small office, the same strength can feel like
too much.

## Who it's for

- Guys who like sweet scents and want to be noticed on a night out.
- Parties, dates and clubs in autumn and winter.
- Anyone who wants a big evening scent without spending much.

Who it's not for: office days, warm weather, or anyone who prefers fresh scents. For something
fresher and more versatile at a similar price, try Armaf Club de Nuit Intense Man (KB-039).
