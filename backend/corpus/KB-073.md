## Short answer

Yes, as long as the order hasn't left our warehouse. Write to support@zoomerfume.example with your
order number and tell us what you'd like to change, or ask us to cancel. Once the order has been
dispatched, it can't be changed.

## Be quick

Orders paid by 15:00 Monday to Friday leave the warehouse the same day, so the window can be short.
Our support team works every day from 9:00 to 21:00 Kyiv time and usually replies within 2 hours.

Zoomer, our AI helper, can't see, change or cancel orders. Please write to support directly, and
always include your order number.

## Before dispatch

You can change the products, the delivery address or the delivery option. You can also cancel the
whole order. If you've already paid, we refund the full amount to the way you paid.

A pre-order can be cancelled any time before it's dispatched, even weeks after you placed it, for a
full refund (see KB-085).

## After dispatch

The order can't be changed or cancelled anymore, but you still have options:

- **Cash on delivery:** you can refuse the parcel at the Nova Poshta branch. Keep in mind that after
  two refusals without a reason, your next order needs prepayment.
- **Prepaid:** collect the parcel, then return the products you don't want within 14 days, unopened
  and with the cellophane intact. You pay for return shipping, and we refund the product price within
  7 working days (see KB-048).

## An example

You order a 50 ml bottle at 10:00 on a Tuesday and then realize you wanted the 100 ml one. Write to
support right away with your order number. If the order hasn't left yet, we change the size before
it ships. If it has already gone, collect the parcel, send the sealed bottle back, and place a new
order for the size you want. We don't do direct exchanges, so a return plus a new order is the way
to swap.

## Ordered over the weekend?

Weekend orders leave on Monday, so you have until Monday's dispatch to change your mind. Write to
support as soon as you can, since we answer every day, weekends included.
