## Short answer

No. We don't sell testers, and we don't sell "unboxed" bottles either. Every perfume we ship is a
new bottle in its original packaging, with a batch code. If you'd like to try a scent before
buying, order a 2 ml sample instead.

## Why not testers?

Testers are made for shops to spray at the counter, and they aren't meant to be sold. They usually
come without the full retail box. We only sell bottles in their original packaging, bought from
official distributors in Ukraine and brand-authorized wholesalers in the EU (see KB-061). Testers
don't fit that rule, so you won't find them in our catalog.

## What you can get instead

- **2 ml sample:** a spray vial of any perfume we have in stock. 69 ₴ for designer and affordable
  perfumes, 119 ₴ for niche ones. Up to 5 samples per order.
- **10 ml decant:** a travel atomizer, for niche perfumes only. The price is on each niche card.
  It's enough to wear a scent for a few weeks before you commit to an expensive bottle.
- **Free sample:** one with every order from 1,500 ₴ (see KB-086).

Samples and decants are filled in our warehouse from our own full bottles, so they're the same
perfume as the bottles we sell. They can't be returned.

## Get the sample price back

If you buy a full bottle of the same perfume within 30 days of buying its sample, the sample price
comes back to you as Zoomer Points. Points need an account, so sign in before you order the bottle.

## An example

You're curious about a niche perfume but don't want to spend thousands on a blind buy. You order a
119 ₴ sample, wear it for a week and fall for it. Three weeks after your sample order, you buy the
full bottle. The 119 ₴ you paid for the sample comes back as 119 points, so the sample pays for
itself.

If you don't like it, you've spent 119 ₴ instead of the price of a bottle you'd never wear.
