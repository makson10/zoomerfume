## Overview

Le Beau Eau de Toilette is Jean Paul Gaultier's summer scent for men. It came out in 2019 and was
created by Quentin Bisch and Sonia Constant. It comes in the brand's torso bottle, here in green
glass wrapped in a leaf. Le Beau is light, creamy and tropical: bergamot, coconut wood and tonka
bean. Don't mix it up with Le Beau Le Parfum, a stronger flanker we don't carry, or with "Le Beau
Male", a name some shops use by mistake. Its sibling in our shop is Le Male Elixir (KB-008), a much
heavier and sweeter evening scent.

## Notes

- **Top:** bergamot
- **Heart:** coconut wood
- **Base:** tonka bean

The brand's description also mentions a touch of geranium. Some lists simply say "coconut" for the
heart.

## How it wears

Fresh bergamot opens it, and within minutes the creamy coconut takes over. It's warm and a little
sweet, like skin after a day at the beach. The tonka bean adds softness in the dry-down. It's a
moderate performer: on our team it lasted 5–7 hours, and the trail softens after the first hour. It
stays pleasant and never gets heavy, even on a hot day. It's one of the softest men's scents we
carry, so it suits people who usually find perfume too much.

## Who it's for

- Spring and summer days, holidays, the beach and relaxed weekends.
- Someone who wants a fresh scent that's soft and easygoing instead of sharp.
- Fans of coconut and tonka who find heavier sweet scents too much in the heat.

Who it's not for: cold evenings, or anyone who wants a strong, long-lasting trail. For that, look at
Jean Paul Gaultier Le Male Elixir (KB-008).

## Zoomer's take

Coconut, sunshine and zero stress. It's the scent of "I'm on vacation" even when it's a Tuesday. Not
a beast, but very easy to love. The 75 ml is 2,290 ₴, so it's one of the easiest summer picks in our
men's range. Wear it with a linen shirt and you're basically on a yacht.
