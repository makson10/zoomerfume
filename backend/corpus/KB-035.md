## Overview

Tobacco Vanille is one of the original Private Blend scents from Tom Ford. It was created by Olivier
Gillotin and launched in 2007. It's a rich, warm blend of pipe tobacco, spice and sweetness, and
for many perfume fans it's the reference point for tobacco scents. It's marketed as unisex. This
card is for the Eau de Parfum. Tom Ford also makes a separate Tobacco Vanille Parfum with different
notes, which we don't carry. Its sibling in our shop is Lost Cherry (KB-034), which is fruity where
this one is smoky. It's one of the niche perfumes we also sell as a 10 ml decant.

## Notes

Tom Ford lists key notes rather than a full pyramid:

- **Key notes:** tobacco leaf, ginger, spice notes, tonka bean, cocoa, fruit accord

Vanilla isn't on Tom Ford's current list, even though it's in the name and comes through clearly on
skin. Fan databases add vanilla, tobacco blossom, dried fruits and woody notes, and leave out
ginger.

## How it wears

The opening is sweet, leafy tobacco with a warm sprinkle of spice and ginger. Within an hour the
cocoa, tonka and vanilla soften it, and the dried-fruit sweetness makes it feel like gingerbread in
a wood-panelled room. It's dense, warm and very strong. On our team it lasted 10 hours or more, with
a big trail for the first few. One or two sprays are enough. More than that can fill a room.

## Who it's for

- Lovers of warm, sweet scents who want something richer than a simple vanilla.
- Cold evenings, holidays and formal nights in late autumn and winter.
- Anyone, of any gender, who wants a scent people remember.

Who it's not for: warm weather, small offices, or anyone who dislikes the smell of tobacco. For a
lighter and much cheaper take on rum, tobacco and vanilla, try Replica Jazz Club (KB-026), from
2,490 ₴ for 30 ml.
