## Overview

Layton is the cold-weather crowd-pleaser from Parfums de Marly. It launched in 2016 and was created by
Hamid Merati-Kashani. It mixes crisp apple and lavender with a warm base of vanilla, spice and woods.
The brand files it under men's scents, though fragrance databases often list it as unisex. Don't
confuse it with Layton Exclusif, a darker version with oud that we don't carry. Layton is one of the
niche perfumes we also sell as a 10 ml decant. Our Parfums de Marly prices were updated on 1 October
2026 (see KB-095).

## Notes

- **Top:** apple, bergamot, cardamom
- **Heart:** lavender, violet, geranium
- **Base:** patchouli, vanilla, guaiac wood, praline

This is the brand's current list. Fragrance databases give a longer one, with mandarin, jasmine,
sandalwood and pepper, and put cardamom in the base.

## How it wears

It opens fresh and juicy: crisp apple and bergamot with a spicy hint of cardamom. Then lavender and
violet make it smooth and slightly floral. Within an hour or two the sweet vanilla and praline base
takes over, warmed by guaiac wood and patchouli. On our team it lasted 8–12 hours, with a strong
trail. It's a powerful perfume, so a couple of sprays are plenty, and in hot weather it can feel like
too much.

## Who it's for

- Someone who wants a warm, sweet signature that gets noticed on dates and nights out.
- Fans of lavender-and-vanilla scents who want a more polished, niche version.
- Autumn and winter: evenings, celebrations and cold-weather weekends.

Who it's not for: anyone who wants something fresh for hot days or a quiet scent for a small office.
For a fresher niche scent, see Creed Aventus (KB-028).

## Zoomer's take

Layton is the "okay, who smells this good?" perfume of cold evenings. Apple, lavender and vanilla sound
simple, but together they're cozy and a little bit dangerous. It's strong, so go easy on the sprays.
