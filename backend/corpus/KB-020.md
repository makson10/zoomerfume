## Overview

Daisy is the Marc Jacobs classic from 2007, created by Alberto Morillas. In 2008 it won two FiFi
Awards, one for the scent and one for its packaging with the daisy-topped cap. It's a sheer, sunny
floral: a fizzy berry opening, soft violet and jasmine, and a clean musky finish. Simple in the best
way. Many flankers share the same shape in other colors, such as Eau So Fresh, Daisy Love, Daisy Dream
and seasonal editions, and some of them smell quite different. We sell the original Daisy Eau de
Toilette.

## Notes

- **Top:** wild berries, white violet
- **Heart:** jasmine
- **Base:** sandalwood

This is the brand's current short list. The original 2007 list, still used by many shops, is longer:
strawberry, violet leaf and grapefruit on top, violet, jasmine and gardenia in the heart, and musk,
white woods and vanilla in the base.

## How it wears

It opens with a sweet-tart sparkle of berries and a little citrus, then softens into violet and
jasmine within half an hour. The finish is clean, slightly creamy musk. It's soft by design: on our
team it lasted 3–5 hours on skin. It's noticeable at arm's length early on, then stays close, which is
why people like it for days spent around others, like classes, work or a busy weekend.

## Who it's for

- Someone new to perfume who wants something easy, cheerful and very hard to dislike.
- A gift for a younger sister, daughter or friend: it's youthful, safe and instantly recognizable.
- Spring and summer days: school or university, the office, brunch and casual plans.

Who it's not for: evenings, winter, or anyone who wants a scent that lasts all day. For something
cozier and longer-lasting at a similar price, try Ariana Grande Cloud (KB-022).
