## Short answer

Yes, to seven countries: Poland, the Czech Republic, Germany, Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia and
Moldova. International parcels go with Nova Post, take 5–10 days and cost a flat 590 ₴. We don't
ship to any other country.

## What it costs

- Delivery is 590 ₴ for every international order, whatever the order size. It's never free.
- Customs fees, if there are any, are yours to pay. They aren't included in our prices.
- All our prices are in UAH. A foreign card is charged in UAH too.

## How to pay

International orders are prepaid only: by card (Visa or Mastercard), Apple Pay or Google Pay. Cash
on delivery works only inside Ukraine, with Nova Poshta.

## How long it takes

Delivery takes 5–10 days, counted from the day the parcel leaves our Kyiv warehouse. Parcels travel
by ground transport only, which is why it takes longer than delivery inside Ukraine.

Orders paid by 15:00 Monday to Friday leave the same day. Later orders leave the next working day,
and weekend orders leave on Monday. As soon as the parcel leaves, you get the Nova Post tracking
number by SMS and email (see KB-072).

## An example

You live in Germany and order a bottle on Wednesday morning, paying by card. The order leaves our
warehouse that day, and the tracking number arrives by SMS and email. You pay the products total
plus 590 ₴ for delivery, and the parcel reaches you within 5–10 days. Any customs fees are paid on
your side.

## Not on the list?

If your country isn't one of the seven, we can't deliver there. You can still send an order to an
address in Ukraine, for example as a gift for someone at home (see KB-080).

## Returns from abroad

The usual returns rules apply: unopened products with the cellophane intact, within 14 days of
receipt. Return shipping is on you (see KB-048). For a damaged or wrong item, write to
support@zoomerfume.example within 3 days of receipt (see KB-050).
